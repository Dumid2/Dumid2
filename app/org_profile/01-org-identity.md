## Mission Statement

> "To empower underserved families to break cycles of intergenerational poverty by providing resources, tools, and holistic support to achieve financial independence and build generational wealth."

---

## Vision Statement

We envision a Southwest DC where a child's future is not determined by their race, income, or address. Where institutions partner with residents instead of policing them. Where young people have clear paths to college, careers, and leadership. And where families build prosperity together, in a community that shows up for one another.

---

## North Star Goal (2030)

Transition 500 low-income Black families in Southwest DC from poverty to sustained household incomes of at least $80,000 per year — up from a current average of approximately $14,000.

---

## Equity Statement (boilerplate)

We operate on the principle that economic mobility requires addressing both skills gaps (financial literacy, entrepreneurship training) and systemic barriers (lack of childcare, mental health challenges, social isolation). We practice equity by eliminating barriers rather than asking people to overcome them. This means our hiring, programming, and partnerships prioritize those with lived experience of the challenges we address. We actively recruit staff and board members from the communities we serve, recognizing that proximity to the problem creates proximity to the solution. Inclusion, for us, means co-creation. We don't design programs for communities; we design them with communities. Our definition of success is determined by the families we serve, not by us.

## Co-Founder & CEO Bio — Darius Baxter

Darius Baxter co-founded GOODProjects in 2016 after graduating from Georgetown University with a degree in Women's and Gender Studies with a concentration in Globalization and Poverty. Raised by a single mother after the murder of his father, and having experienced homelessness, Baxter has directed $8M+ in community investment into Southwest DC and abroad. He worked on Capitol Hill for Congresswoman Eleanor Holmes Norton and was elected as one of the youngest delegates to the 2016 Democratic National Convention. He launched the Baxter Family Kids Centre in Nairobi, Kenya in 2019 and the Black Justice Fund in 2025 with an initial $150,000 commitment. He serves on the board of DC Central Kitchen and has partnered with the NFL Players Association, New Balance, and Wondros. ED start date: January 2, 2016.
