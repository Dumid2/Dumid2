**Note on DC Commission on the Arts & Humanities' Field Trip Experiences
grant:** that program requires all submitted programming to take place
within DC, with deviations disqualifying the application. Of the
arts/humanities-type trips above (Wolf Trap, AnnMarie Sculpture Garden,
Creative Cauldron), none are in DC. Kraken Kourts & Skates is in DC but
is recreational (skating), not arts/humanities content. If GOODProjects
wants to pursue this specific grant, it would need to be built around a
new, DC-based arts/humanities field trip (e.g., a Smithsonian museum,
the Kennedy Center, or Arena Stage, which is itself in Southwest DC)
rather than the trips already on this calendar.
