## Framing

10 years of GOODProjects (founded 2016). Positioning line: "A Decade of
Impact, A Vision for Scale," working toward 2030 goals. The four-phase
continuum is pitched as **one seamless ecosystem**, not four separate
programs — use this framing in executive summaries rather than describing
the phases as standalone programs competing for the same dollar.

## Four-Phase Continuum (as named in this brief)

| Phase | Program | Serves | Core function |
|---|---|---|---|
| I — Stabilize | Family Success Planning (FSP) | Parents/caregivers; youth 14–24 | Dedicated Family Success Coach + customized plan: emergency stabilization, benefits navigation, financial literacy, employment support |
| II — Protect | GOODLearningHub Afterschool | Youth 5–14 | High-dosage tutoring (UFLI Foundations, Science of Reading, IXL), Youth Services Advocate (YSA), SEL, peace circles, daily family touchpoints |
| III — Intervene | GOODZone Violence Interruption | Young adults 18–35; high-risk community members | Street-level outreach, conflict mediation, 48-hour rapid crisis response after any shooting, Credible Messengers |
| IV — Employ | JUMPSTART: Youth Workforce Development | GOODLearningHub alumni 14–24 | Alumni tracking, Career Launchpad training, paid internship with GOODZone Business Partners, permanent job transition, 90-day retention support |

## JUMPSTART — Four-Stage Alumni Pathway

1. **Alumni Tracking & Intake** (Weeks 1–2): outreach, intake assessment, dedicated coach assigned.
2. **Career Launchpad Training** (Weeks 3–4): 40-hour curriculum — resume building, interview coaching, professional communication, financial literacy, workplace etiquette, SEL.
3. **Paid Internship Placement** (Weeks 5–10): placed with a GOODZone Business Partner, **$15/hour, 20 hrs/week for six weeks**, weekly trauma-informed wellness check-ins.
4. **Placement & 90-Day Retention** (Weeks 11–24): Placement Coordinator supports transition to permanent hire; retention tracked.

"Two-Generation Anchor": while the youth moves through JUMPSTART, the
parent/caregiver stays enrolled in FSP — pitched as the single most
powerful predictor of successful youth employment transitions.

## Unique Impact Indicators (use these to differentiate from generic workforce-program metrics)

- **Youth-to-Mentor Conversion Rate** — JUMPSTART alumni who return as paid peer mentors/camp counsellors/youth advocates within 12 months.
- **Household Income Trajectory (Two-Generation Impact)** — % of families where both youth AND parent/caregiver saw increased earned income or secure employment during the grant period.
- **Relational Trust Retention Rate** — % of JUMPSTART participants still actively engaged with GOODProjects services 6+ months after internship placement, regardless of employment outcome.
- **Street Network Disengagement Rate** — % of youth self-reporting reduced contact with street-involved peers/criminal activity at 90-day follow-up vs. intake.
- **Employer Repeat Hire Rate** — % of GOODZone Business Partners who hire a second alumnus within the grant period after a successful first placement.

## Measurable Outcome Targets (JUMPSTART)

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Alumni complete 40-hour Career Launchpad Training | 90% |
| Alumni complete 6-week paid internship | 85% |
| Alumni secure unsubsidized employment or post-secondary enrollment within 90 days | 60% |
| Internship-to-permanent-hire conversion rate | 40% |
| Employers report measurable soft-skill improvement (pre/post survey) | 75% |

Secondary metrics tracked: average hourly wage at placement; employer
retention at 6 and 12 months; youth self-reported professional confidence
scores; parent household income trajectory through FSP.

## Proven Track Record (cite as prior-performance evidence)

- **Peer-to-Peer Mentoring (2021–present):** 50+ youth served as paid/volunteer "Free Talk with a Trusted Friend" peer mentors.
- **Nasdaq Women's Financial Literacy Program (2025):** 15 women completed; all explored entrepreneurship; 2 launched registered businesses in Ward 6.
- **SWBID Partnership:** 5 youth annually placed in paid stipend positions within GOODProjects — direct precursor to the employer-placement model.
- **D.O.E.S. Collaboration:** active partnership aligning workforce pathways with District youth employment initiatives.
- **Overall alumni engagement:** 100+ youth alumni engaged in paid/volunteer roles with zero incidents of violence or program disruption.

## Key Partnerships

- **GOODZone Business Partners (Ward 6)** — paid internships, permanent hire opportunities.

## Funding Structure — Full Continuum

- **Full continuum annual programming cost: $1.8 million (approximately $2 million)** — this is the whole four-phase ecosystem, not just JUMPSTART. Use for "total program cost" framing, not as a single grant ask.
- JUMPSTART is explicitly scalable by funding level:

| Funding Scenario | Youth Served | Staffing |
|---|---|---|
| Full Implementation | 30 youth | Workforce Development Coach (1.0 FTE), Internship & Placement Coordinator (1.0 FTE), SEL & Wellness Coach (0.5 FTE), Program Manager (0.15 FTE shared) |
| Scaled Implementation | 15–20 youth | Shared staffing with existing FSP/GOODZone infrastructure |

### JUMPSTART per-youth costs (use for budget narratives/justifications)

- Paid internship stipends: **$1,800/youth** (6 weeks × 20 hrs/week × $15/hr)
- Career Launchpad materials: **$50/youth**
- Transportation (MetroCards): **$50/youth**
- Wellness support: **$150/youth**
- Staff salaries: **$162,000** for full implementation (30 youth)

## 2030 Vision (for sustainability / long-term-impact narrative sections)

- Deepen the evidence base via systematic SROI tracking, alumni follow-up, longitudinal data.
- Build a data-driven donor case from verified numbers and independent impact validation.
- Fully operationalize JUMPSTART as the standing fourth phase of the continuum for every GOODLearningHub alumnus.
