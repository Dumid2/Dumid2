# Development Division — Team Structure & Responsibilities

> Source: "GOODProjects Development Division: Team Structure &
> Responsibilities" (effective immediately, FY2026). This is internal
> operating structure, not proposal content — but it directly defines how
> grant-writing work at GOODProjects is supposed to happen, including the
> Grant Writer role this tool is built to support. Keep this current if the
> Development Division's structure changes.

**Goal of the division:** "Bring more resources into the organization
while providing exceptional service and stewardship to those who already
support our work." Responsible for securing, stewarding, and reporting on
the resources necessary to support GOODProjects' mission and growth.

## Roles

### Neo — Development Lead
Owns the overall fundraising pipeline, relationship strategy, and
Development Team management.

- **Pipeline & Strategy:** identify/pursue new funding opportunities,
  develop fundraising strategy/priorities, review major grant submissions
  before final approval.
- **Relationships:** build/maintain relationships with foundations,
  corporations, government agencies, individual donors; coordinate with
  Executive Leadership on fundraising goals.
- **Team Management:** manage Development Division workflows, supervise
  team members, ensure capacity alignment and productivity.
- **Success metrics:** Dollars Submitted, Dollars Awarded, Opportunities
  Identified, Engagement & Retention (funder engagement/renewal),
  Team Performance.

(Note: this document's title for Neo — "Development Lead" — is a
different label than "Fundraising & Grants Officer" used in
`03-staff-board.md`. Same person/role, different label across documents;
use whichever the current org chart calls it if a funder needs an exact
title.)

### Dumi — Grant Writer (this role)
Responsible for producing high-quality grant proposals, applications, and
funding submissions across foundation, corporate, government, and federal
sources. **Squarely focused on execution and production** — turning
opportunities Neo identifies into polished, compelling submissions.

- **Proposal Production:** draft grant proposals/applications; prepare
  foundation/corporate/government/federal submissions; use existing
  GOODProjects materials, reports, and templates; **"leverage AI tools to
  improve efficiency and output quality"** (this is the explicit
  organizational mandate this tool exists to fulfill); support new program
  concept development when opportunities require it.
- **Pipeline & Deadlines:** maintain the grant submission calendar; track
  proposal progress/deadlines; attend grant webinars/technical assistance
  sessions; communicate proactively with Neo on submission timelines.
- **Success metrics:** Volume & Timeliness (grants submitted, on-time
  rate), Quality & Impact (proposal quality scores, award rate achieved),
  Turnaround Time (assignment to draft completion).
- **Reports directly to Neo.**

### Melissa — Reporting & Stewardship Manager
Ensures GOODProjects fulfills all obligations to existing funders, donors,
and partners — the "organizational backbone of funder trust."

- **Reporting & Compliance:** maintain master reporting calendar; track
  all grant/donor reporting deadlines; draft grant reports and funder
  updates; coordinate with Operations and Finance for required data;
  ensure all reporting obligations are completed accurately and on time.
- **Stewardship & Relationships:** maintain regular funder/donor
  communication; support individual giving stewardship; schedule/
  participate in stewardship meetings; track donor engagement/follow-up.
- **Success metrics:** On-Time Reporting Rate, Funder & Donor Retention
  (year-over-year), Accuracy & Touchpoints.
- **Reports directly to the Development Lead (Neo).**

## How the team works together (the full cycle)

**Identify** (Neo spots funding opportunities) → **Prepare** (Dumi drafts
and submits proposals) → **Award** (grant awarded, funds received) →
**Maintain** (Melissa manages reporting and relationships) → **Renewal**
(pursue renewal or next cycle).

"No single team member owns the entire process. The Development Division
operates as an integrated unit — each role feeds the next, creating a
complete and self-reinforcing fundraising cycle."

## Escalation expectations

Neo must be informed **immediately** — not after deadlines have passed or
relationships have been strained — when any of the following arise:

- Missed or at-risk deadlines
- Delayed or incomplete reports
- Funder concerns or complaints
- Submission risks or blockers
- Capacity challenges
- Opportunities requiring executive involvement

## FY2026 Division Goals

Identify Opportunities (consistently surface qualified funding prospects
across all categories) · High-Quality Submissions (compelling, accurate
proposals reflecting the mission with excellence) · Excellent
Relationships (strong, trust-based connections with every funder/donor) ·
Increase Revenue (grow annual funding while meeting all reporting
requirements on time) · Scalable Systems (workflows/infrastructure that
grow with the organization without sacrificing quality or relationships).
