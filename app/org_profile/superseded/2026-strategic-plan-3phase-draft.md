# **GOODProjects: A Decade of Impact, A Vision for Scale**

### **2026 Strategic Brief \-  Towards Our 2030 Goals**

---

## **1\. Who We Are & The 10-Year Foundation**

GOODProjects is a Washington, D.C.-based nonprofit founded in 2016 with a singular mission: empower underserved families to break cycles of intergenerational poverty by providing resources, tools, and holistic support to achieve financial independence and build generational wealth.

Over the past decade, we have developed an integrated, end-to-end continuum of care that surrounds families with support at every stage from the front door of social services to the safe spaces of after-school programming to the street corners where violence disrupts lives. This is not a collection of isolated programs. It is a single, seamless ecosystem of care that has been refined, tested, and proven in Ward 6 over ten years.

---

## **2\. The Depth of Our Impact \-  What 10 Years Has Built**

Our three-phase model has produced measurable, validated results that demonstrate the depth of long-term, holistic intervention:

**Violence Interruption:**

* 45% reduction in violent crime in PSA 105 (2023–2024), compared to a 12% increase citywide, validated by independent MPD data analysis.  
* Over six years, the GOODZone recorded only 249 violent incidents, compared with 601 in the neighbouring Navy Yard area.

**Family Stabilisation:**

* 300+ families stabilised annually through FSP coaching, benefits navigation, and economic mobility support.

**Youth Development:**

* 400+ youth served daily at King Greenleaf Recreation Centre through high-dosage tutoring, mentorship, and enrichment.

**Alumni & SROI Tracking (2026 Priority):** As one of our critical focuses for 2026 is systematically tracking the alumni families and young people GOODProjects has served. Documenting their current trajectories across employment, educational attainment, housing stability, and financial independence. These long-term outcomes represent the truest measure of sustainable, generational change.

As part of this effort, we are expanding workforce readiness programming for opportunity youth ages 16–24, ensuring that the gains made during their time with GOODProjects are reinforced and built upon \- not lost to the pressures of economic instability or community disinvestment. This continuity of support is critical to protecting the progress our families have worked hard to achieve.

The Social Return on Investment (SROI) data generated through this tracking initiative will serve as the cornerstone of our donor engagement and advocacy strategy, translating lived impact into compelling evidence for continued investment in Southwest D.C.'s most resilient families.

*Numbers don't lie. Our alumni stories will demonstrate that the change we create flows back into the community.*

## **3\. The Model \-  How the Continuum Works**

Every phase of our continuum is rooted in the Family Success Planning (FSP) framework, ensuring no participant falls through the cracks.

### **Phase I: Entry Point \-  Family Success Planning (FSP)**

The cycle of intergenerational poverty cannot be interrupted without first stabilising the home. A child cannot learn if they are hungry, housing insecure, or witnessing trauma.

Each family is paired with a dedicated **Family Success Coach** who builds a customised plan addressing:

* **Immediate Stabilisation:** Emergency food, housing, utility assistance; ID recovery; benefits navigation (Medicaid, SNAP, TANF); behavioural health and primary care connections; childcare support.  
* **Economic Mobility:** Workforce readiness, job placement, financial literacy coaching, entrepreneurship training, and wealth-building tools, including whole-life insurance policies.  
* **Youth Engagement:** Automatic enrolment of school-aged children into GOODLearningHub; access to therapy, Social-Emotional Learning, and mentoring.

### **Phase II: Safe Harbour \-  GOODLearningHub Afterschool Services**

The hours between 3:30 pm and 6:30 pm are when youth are most at risk. The GOODLearningHub, housed at King Greenleaf Recreation Centre, provides:

* **Academic Support:** High-dosage tutoring in literacy and math using evidence-based curricula (UFLI Foundations, Science of Reading, IXL adaptive learning); small-group and one-on-one instruction; real-time progress monitoring.  
* **Mentorship & Social-Emotional Support:** Each youth is assigned a Youth Services Advocate (YSA) who monitors attendance, liaises with teachers, and provides social-emotional coaching.  
* **Family Connection:** Daily touchpoints between YSAs and families, creating a continuous feedback loop between the home and the hub.

### **Phase III: Community Shield \-  GOODZone Violence Interruption**

Violence is a public health crisis driven by unaddressed trauma, idleness, and economic despair. The GOODZone is the outward-facing, mobile arm of the continuum, operating across Ward 6 and Ward 8\.

The GOODZone team, comprising Credible Messengers, Crisis Intervention Specialists, and Outreach Workers, delivers:

* **Street-Level Outreach & Conflict Mediation:** Daily presence in identified hotspots; real-time dispute mediation to prevent retaliation.  
* **Rapid Crisis Response:** Within 48 hours of any shooting or major conflict, the team activates to stabilise individuals and families, provide trauma counselling, and link them to FSP coaches.  
* **Workforce & Income Stabilisation:** On-the-ground connections to job placements, ID recovery, and immediate needs \- replacing idling with purpose and a pathway.

## **4\. The Integrated Ecosystem in Action**

**A Real-World Example:**

* **Ms. Jones** (FSP): Referred by her resident council leader, her Family Success Coach secures childcare subsidies, Medicaid enrollment, ID recovery, and financial literacy training. Her children are enrolled in the GOODLearningHub.  
* **Marcus, age 10** (GOODLearningHub): His YSA identifies that he is withdrawn and struggling with reading. Targeted tutoring and peace circles improve his grades, confidence, and social connections.  
* **Terrell, age 19** (GOODZone): Ms. Jones's nephew, identified as being involved in a neighbourhood conflict, is engaged by a Credible Messenger. Existing trust built through his family's connection to GOODProjects \- enables mediation. Terrell connects with a Crisis Intervention Specialist, then an FSP Coach, and is supported to obtain his GED, enrol in a hospitality training programme, and open a savings account.

**The Outcome:** Ms. Jones is on a path to financial independence. Marcus is reading at grade level. Terrell is employed and disconnected from the street economy. A neighbourhood conflict was mediated before it turned deadly.

## **5\. The 2030 Vision \- Scale & Replication**

With a decade of proof in Ward 6, GOODProjects is ready to scale. Our 2030 goals are:

1. **Replicate the model** to additional wards, bringing the same integrated continuum of care to new communities across Washington, D.C.  
2. **Deepen the evidence base** through systematic SROI tracking, alumni follow-up, and longitudinal data collection that demonstrates sustainable, generational change.  
3. **Build a data-driven donor case** \- using verified numbers, alumni outcomes, and independent impact validation to attract and retain funders who are aligned with long-term community transformation.
