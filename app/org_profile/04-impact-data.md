## Violence Interruption Data

- **45% reduction** in violent crime in PSA 105 (2023–2024) — validated by independent MPD data analysis — compared to a **12% increase citywide** during the same period
- Over six years, the GOODZone recorded only **249 violent incidents**, compared with **601 in the neighboring Navy Yard area**
- Crisis response delivered within **48 hours of any shooting**
- 186 community members engaged in violence prevention and trauma response (FY25)

**Source:** MPD data; GOODProjects internal tracking; Crime_Data_Comparison_GOODZone_2019_2025.pdf
