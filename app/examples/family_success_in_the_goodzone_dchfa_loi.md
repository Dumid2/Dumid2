## Program model as described here

Each family gets a **Family Success Coach** who assesses across **10
domains**: income, employment, education, housing, health, mental
wellness, safety, social support, life skills, community engagement —
then builds a customized Family Success Plan, monitored quarterly via
dashboards. Youth get academic support/character development via GOODCamp
and afterschool tutoring.

**Goals stated:** serve 150+ residents across Ward 6; increase household
income stability/workforce participation; improve academic/SEL outcomes
for youth; strengthen community cohesion via family workshops/events.

## Implementation timeline (reusable structure for a 12-month resident-services grant)

1. **Month 1 — Enrollment & Assessment:** referrals from schools/resident
   councils/partners; baseline Family Success Assessments.
2. **Months 1–2 — Planning & Goal Setting:** SMART goals across the 10
   domains.
3. **Months 3–12 — Service Delivery:** coaching, youth enrichment,
   community linkages, monthly progress reviews.
4. **Ongoing — Monitoring & Evaluation:** dashboard tracking + participant
   surveys.

## Evaluation benchmarks used in this LOI

- 80% of families achieve progress in at least 5 of the 10 domains within
  12 months.
- 70% of youth show academic or behavioral improvement after 6 months.
- 90% of participants report greater community connection/confidence.

## Sustainability narrative used here (notable, somewhat unusual framing)

This LOI names three specific revenue-diversification mechanisms as the
"beyond this grant" sustainability story:
- **Medicaid partnerships** reimbursing community-based case management
  and behavioral health support (recurring revenue).
- **Whole life insurance-backed Family Trusts** — GOODProjects allocates
  a portion of earned surplus to fund policies for participating
  families, building intergenerational wealth while creating long-term
  reinvestment capacity for the org. (This also appears as a service
  offered to families under FSP's "Economic Mobility" component in
  `../org_profile/06-strategic-brief-2026.md`, so it's a consistent,
  real mechanism — not unique to this document.)
- **Corporate/philanthropic partnerships** matching public investment.
