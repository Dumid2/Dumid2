# Model Narrative: FSP / Victim Services Project Description (OVSJG-style)

> Source: `FSP_Project_Description__Narrative.docx`. A full project
> narrative written for a DC Office of Victim Services and Justice Grants
> (OVSJG)-style RFA — victim services / trauma-recovery framing for the
> Family Success Planning program. Use this as a **model/template for
> future victim-services or trauma-informed submissions**, not as a
> reusable boilerplate to paste verbatim — the specific targets (35
> families by June 2026, 65 youth) were written for a particular grant
> cycle and need updating for any new submission.

## Structure this narrative uses (mirror this section order for similar RFAs)

1. **Need for the Project (with local data):** cites Ward 6 gun-violence
   increase (25% YoY per MPD), specific at-risk communities (King
   Greenleaf, James Creek, Syphax Gardens).
2. **Target Population & Geographic Area:** Black families in Ward 6,
   crime victims and secondary survivors (children, caregivers, extended
   family).
3. **Proposed Activities:** FSP trauma-informed assessments/safety
   planning (within 2 weeks of intake), crisis intervention/advocacy,
   resource navigation, GOODLearningHub youth programming, SW Peace
   Gardens activation.
4. **Service Areas:** advocacy/case management, crisis counseling,
   housing assistance/safety planning, community outreach, trauma-informed
   mental health referrals, victim data collection/reporting.
5. **Goals/Objectives/Timeline** (this cycle's specific targets — update
   per grant period):
   - Goal 1: FSP for **35 families by June 2026**.
   - Goal 2: **65 youth** in year-round GOODLearningHub programming.
   - Goal 3: maintain/expand SW Peace Gardens; **4 community events**
     focused on trauma recovery, healing circles, violence prevention.
6. **Evaluation:** quarterly reporting in GovGrants; enrollment counts,
   pre/post youth well-being surveys, Peace Garden participation, victim
   service demographics.
7. **Mission Alignment:** ties back to "help 500 families in Southwest DC
   achieve self-sufficiency by 2030" — this matches the "500 families to
   sustainable household incomes by 2030" goal already in Darius Baxter's
   grant bio in `03-staff-board.md`, so it's a consistent organizational
   target, not a new conflicting figure. (This is a separate thing from
   the mission-*statement* wording, which does still have three unresolved
   variants — see `../compliance/financial_and_compliance_facts.md`.)
8. **Role in the Victim Services Continuum:** positions GOODProjects
   within DC's broader referral network (housing, legal aid, health
   clinics, DC agencies).
9. **Evidence Base:** cites National Child Traumatic Stress Network
   (NCTSN), Office for Victims of Crime (OVC), Afterschool Alliance, RAND
   Corporation as the research base for the FSP/case-management and
   out-of-school-time models.
10. **Trauma-Informed Principles:** safety, trustworthiness, peer
    support, collaboration, empowerment, cultural humility — staff named
    as trained in Trauma-Informed Care: **Marquel Webb** (FSP Program
    Manager) and **Stephanie Page-Baxter** (Youth Program Director).
    (Title note: this document calls Marquel Webb "Family Success
    Planning Program Manager" and Stephanie Page-Baxter "Youth Program
    Director" — `03-staff-board.md` uses "Family Success Planning Lead"
    and "Director of Youth Development." Same people, minor title
    variance across documents; use whichever title `03-staff-board.md`
    has as the current canonical one unless told otherwise.)
11. **Community-Informed Design:** listening sessions with Syphax
    Gardens/Greenleaf Resident Council Presidents; ongoing Family
    Advisory Council and Youth Council (compensated, with real
    decision-making authority — good specific detail for "community
    voice" sections funders increasingly require).
12. **Organizational Experience:** cites 2023–2024 track record — FSP to
    **56 families**, 60+ youth via GOODLearningHub, healing events at SW
    Peace Gardens.
13. **Staff bios used in this narrative:**
    - **Marquel Webb** — BA in Administration; certified Trauma-Informed
      Care, Suicide Prevention, "Too Good For Drugs" (certified 9/3/2024).
    - **Stephanie Page-Baxter** — Early Childhood Learning & Positive
      Youth Development background; certified Trauma-Informed Care,
      Suicide Prevention, Human Sexuality, "Too Good For Drugs."
    - **Phileke Holland** — GOODLearningHub Instructional Support
      Coordinator, Psychology senior at Towson University.
14. **Financial/Administrative Systems:** 501(c)(3), QuickBooks Online,
    board-reviewed internal controls.
15. **Data & Evaluation:** unique participant identifiers, data
    disaggregated by race/ethnicity/gender/victimization type (OVSJG
    standard), monthly staff review + quarterly leadership review.

## Reusable case example

Ms. S — single mother of three in Southwest DC, unemployed and facing
eviction when she joined FSP in 2023; started a business and secured
housing within six months; children enrolled in GOODCamp; now volunteers
as a peer mentor. (This same case example also appears, differently
framed, in `../org_profile/06-strategic-brief-2026.md`'s Ms. Jones/Marcus/
Terrell case study — these may be composite or illustrative examples
rather than the literal same family; don't assume they're identical
without checking with staff.)
