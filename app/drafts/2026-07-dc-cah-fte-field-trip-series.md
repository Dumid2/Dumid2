## SCOPE OF WORK / PROJECT NARRATIVE

### Program Purpose and Goals

GOODProjects requests $13,000 to deliver a three-part visual arts field trip series for GOODLearningHub's 3rd through 5th grade students: the Smithsonian American Art Museum and Renwick Gallery, the National Gallery of Art, and the Anacostia Community Museum, each paired with a pre-trip classroom workshop, a post-trip reflection session, and a professional development session for Youth Services Advocates on integrating visual arts into afterschool academic support. All three destinations sit within the District of Columbia. This directly matches CAH's FTE goals of providing comprehensive arts and humanities field trip experiences, taking a strategic approach to planning and delivering a series of experiences rather than a single outing, and making authentic curricular connections in collaboration with DCPS.

### Target Population

Residents of James Creek, Syphax Gardens, and Greenleaf public housing communities in Southwest DC's Ward 6, enrolled in GOODLearningHub, with participating students drawn primarily from GOODProjects' partnership with Amidon-Bowen Elementary School.
