# Triage notes — Regions Bank, PHTC, Antioch Foundation, Gertrude L. Hirsch Charitable Trust

Requested alongside the National Park Trust strengthening (July 13, 2026). All
four were checked against the funder's own current guidelines before
drafting, per the playbook's qualify-first step. Three come back as
no-go or not-actionable today; none were drafted as narratives.

## Row 215 — Regions Bank Matching Gifts Program: NOT A WRITTEN PROPOSAL

Confirmed directly from Regions' own community engagement page
(regions.com/about-regions/community-engagement/community-partners-and-associates).
The "Matching Gifts Program" specifically requires an existing **full-time
Regions associate or a retiree within five years of retirement** to
initiate the match request — GOODProjects cannot submit a proposal
into this program on its own. There is no application for a nonprofit
to write.

The same page does list a separate "Regions Bank Contributions &
Sponsorships" channel where community partners submit proposals directly,
and a Regions Foundation channel that is invitation-only. If GOODProjects
wants to pursue Regions Bank as a funder, that Contributions & Sponsorships
channel is the real path, but it is a different, untracked opportunity
that needs its own research and its own row, not a rewrite of this one.

**Recommendation:** close this row as not-applicable in its current form.
If there's a known Regions associate connected to GOODProjects (board
member, donor, staff), route a match request through them directly rather
than a written application. Flagging to Neo to decide whether to open a
new row for the Contributions & Sponsorships channel instead.

## Row 218 — Public Health Training Centers (PHTC) Program: confirms prior NO-GO

This is the same conclusion the batch triage already reached, now
double-checked directly against HRSA's Bureau of Health Workforce page
(bhw.hrsa.gov/programs/public-health-training-centers-phtc, portal open
through July 17, 2026). PHTC funds regional Public Health Training Center
consortia built to train the credentialed public health workforce —
in practice, applicants are schools/programs of public health or academic
public health training centers, not community-based direct-service
nonprofits.

Worth naming precisely: there is a *different* HRSA opportunity, the
Community Health Worker Training Program (CHWTP, HRSA-22-124), that does
explicitly welcome community-based organizations as eligible applicants.
That is not the opportunity tracked in row 218, though — it is a separate
NOFO with its own number, cycle, and (per HRSA's FAQ) its own accreditation
requirements for the certificate-training component. If GOODProjects wants
to pursue CHW training funding, that would need to be opened as its own
opportunity and researched on its own timeline, not folded into this
July 17 PHTC deadline.

**Recommendation:** stands as a no-go for row 218 specifically. Confirm
with Neo whether the CHWTP angle is worth a fresh look as a separate
opportunity; it is not a same-week task given accreditation documentation
requirements that would need lead time to assemble.

## Row 222 — Antioch Foundation Grant: NO-GO FOR NOW (wrong cycle)

Confirmed directly from antiochfoundation.org: **the 2027 grant cycle
application window is September 15 to October 15, 2026.** There is no
open application today, so nothing can be submitted this week regardless
of fit. The foundation's stated mission is explicitly faith-based
("the areas of faith, education, medicine, and humanitarian need,"
framed around Christian scripture and the founding of Antioch as an
early center of Gentile Christianity) — worth a fit conversation with
Neo on whether GOODProjects positions itself as a faith-adjacent
applicant under the education or humanitarian-need pillars, separate
from the timing issue.

**Recommendation:** not a this-week task. If Neo wants to pursue it,
calendar it for the September 15, 2026 window and revisit the
faith-alignment question then.

## Row 228 — Gertrude L. Hirsch Charitable Trust: NO-GO (geography and deadline both fail)

Confirmed directly from Wells Fargo's foundation grants page
(wellsfargo.com/private-foundations/hirsch-charitable-trust). Two
disqualifying findings:

1. **Geography:** "States served: New Jersey," with "a practice of
   supporting organizations located in the New Jersey area" and explicit
   preference for New Jersey-based applicants — the trust traces to
   Gertrude Hirsch's residence in Ho-Ho-Kus, Bergen County, NJ. GOODProjects
   is a DC-only organization with no New Jersey presence. This is the
   same kind of geographic dealbreaker already flagged for International
   Paper, Corning, and Entergy in the batch triage.
2. **Deadline:** the trust's real annual deadline is **July 7**, not
   July 13 as shown in the dev bible — meaning the actual window closed
   six days ago, independent of the geography problem. Worth correcting
   the dev bible's date for this row so it doesn't resurface as an active
   item next cycle.

Average grant size is also small ($5,000–$10,000) relative to the effort
of a full application.

**Recommendation:** close as a no-go on both geography and expired
deadline. Correct the July 13 due date in the dev bible to July 7 (annual)
for future reference.

## Net result

Of the four requested, none were drafted as narratives — Regions Bank
and PHTC are the wrong mechanism/entity type, Antioch's window isn't open
yet, and Gertrude Hirsch fails geography with an already-passed real
deadline. Flagging all four to Neo for confirmation rather than sinking
drafting time into applications that can't be submitted or won't qualify.
