GOODProjects Grant Qualification Note
Row 211, 2026 dev bible tab: Capitol Hill Community Foundation — Keller and Franzén Grants

## Decision: NO-GO (flagged, not drafted)

## Why

The Capitol Hill Community Foundation's published guidelines state it only
considers applications that benefit **the Capitol Hill community**
specifically. GOODProjects' service area is Southwest DC's Ward 6 public
housing communities (James Creek, Syphax Gardens, Greenleaf, King
Greenleaf Recreation Center), which is a distinct DC neighborhood from
Capitol Hill. Both fall within the District, but the foundation's stated
geographic scope is narrower than "DC" or even "Ward 6" broadly.

## What was found

- **Keller Grant:** a single $25,000/year grant supporting the creation or
  expansion of major initiatives with potential to significantly benefit
  the Capitol Hill community specifically. Preference given to
  organizations with prior Foundation support.
- **Franzén Grant for the Arts:** supports visual/performing/architectural
  and design arts programs on Capitol Hill.
- **Application format:** a letter of no more than four pages, emailed to
  weinheimer@aol.com or mailed to Mark Weinheimer, Chair, CHCF Grants
  Committee, 419 East Capitol Street SE, Washington, DC 20003.
- **Deadline:** the most recently published cycle's letter-of-intent
  deadline was Monday, October 27, 2025, 5:00 p.m., with awards announced
  around February and presented in spring. No 2026 cycle deadline was
  found, so it is unclear whether there is a currently open cycle to
  target, or whether the entry in the dev bible is tracking a past/future
  cycle.

## Recommendation

Skip this opportunity unless Neo has specific knowledge of a GOODProjects
program or partnership that operates within the Capitol Hill neighborhood
itself, or a personal relationship with the Foundation that would make an
exception plausible. If Neo wants to pursue it anyway, the next step
would be confirming the current cycle's deadline and geographic
flexibility directly with Mark Weinheimer before any drafting time is
spent.
