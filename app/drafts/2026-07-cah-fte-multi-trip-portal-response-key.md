**What is the organization's mission and vision?** (200 word limit)

GOODProjects designs opportunities for youth and their families to live
fulfilling lives, free from poverty and violence, with improvements to their
health and wellness. Founded in Washington, DC in 2016, GOODProjects operates an
integrated, two-generation continuum of care centered at King Greenleaf
Recreation Center in Southwest DC's Ward 6. Family Success Planning stabilizes
parents and caregivers through benefits navigation, financial coaching, and
crisis response. GOODLearningHub provides year-round afterschool and summer
enrichment, tutoring, and social-emotional learning for youth ages 5 to 14.
GOODZone interrupts community violence through street-level outreach and rapid
crisis response. JUMPSTART moves GOODLearningHub alumni into paid internships
and permanent employment. Each phase feeds the next, so progress compounds
across a family rather than resetting with each new program year.

Our vision is a Southwest DC where a child's future is not shaped by race,
income, or address, where institutions partner with residents instead of
policing them, where young people have clear paths to college and careers, and
where families build prosperity together. By 2030, GOODProjects aims to move
500 low-income Black families in Southwest DC to sustained household incomes of
$80,000 or more.

**Top three accomplishments from the past two years** (400 word limit)

Over the past two years, GOODProjects has driven measurable results across its
continuum of care in Southwest DC.

First, GOODProjects' GOODZone violence interruption model recorded a 45 percent
reduction in violent crime in Police Service Area 105 between 2023 and 2024,
independently validated against Metropolitan Police Department data, compared
with a 12 percent citywide increase over the same period. Over the past six
years, GOODZone's footprint recorded only 249 violent incidents, compared with
601 in the neighboring Navy Yard area, a result achieved through Credible
Messenger outreach, conflict mediation, and crisis response within 48 hours of
any shooting.

Second, GOODProjects' Family Success Planning program stabilized more than 300
families annually through dedicated Family Success Coaches, benefits
navigation, financial literacy coaching, and crisis intervention. In FY25
alone, FSP delivered more than $100,000 in direct support to families facing
eviction, violence, or financial emergency, and connected every enrolled
family's school-aged children directly into GOODLearningHub.

Third, GOODProjects' GOODLearningHub served more than 400 youth daily at King
Greenleaf Recreation Center through high-dosage tutoring, Youth Services
Advocates, and social-emotional learning. In FY25, GOODLearningHub delivered
264 tutoring sessions, answered 174,653 academic questions, and helped students
master 1,912 skills across core subjects, all while providing daily meals and
on-site mental health services to a population living below 200 percent of the
federal poverty line.

Together, these three results demonstrate why GOODProjects functions as the
"community quarterback" for Southwest DC's public housing communities: a single
organization stabilizing the home, developing the child, and interrupting
violence on the street, so that gains in one domain reinforce the others.

(Figures above are drawn from GOODProjects' 2026 Pitch Deck, treated as
authoritative organizational canon per standing practice, and reconciled
against the FY25 program metrics in `04-impact-data.md`.)
