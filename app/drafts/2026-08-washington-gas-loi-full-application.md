## PART A: LETTER OF INTENT (LOI)

### Opening Paragraph: Mission and Ask

GOODProjects operates GOODZone, a violence interruption program that has kept violent crime in Southwest DC's public housing communities at less than half the level of a comparable neighboring area over the past six years. We respectfully request $12,000 from Washington Gas's Community Investment Program to fund two additional Community Outreach Workers for one year, extending GOODZone's daily street-level presence in Ward 6's highest-risk corridors.

### The Problem We Are Solving

Southwest DC's public housing communities, including Greenleaf Gardens, Syphax Gardens, and the corridors around Buzzard Point and the Waterfront Metro, have experienced sustained gun violence for years. Over the past six years, GOODZone's coverage area recorded 249 violent incidents, compared with 601 in the neighboring Navy Yard area, a rate less than half as high despite serving a population with lower income and higher poverty rates. Violent crime in the GOODZone peaked at 53 incidents in 2023 before falling to 29 in 2024, a decline achieved through daily outreach, not through the crime falling on its own. Sustaining that decline requires continued street-level presence, and current funding covers a smaller outreach team than the corridors require.

### Our Approach

GOODZone deploys Credible Messengers, Outreach Workers, and Crisis Intervention Specialists, all with direct lived experience of violence and incarceration, for daily street-level presence in identified hotspot corridors: M Street SE, Buzzard Point, Syphax Gardens, Greenleaf Gardens, and the Waterfront Metro area. The team mediates conflicts in real time to prevent retaliation, and activates within 48 hours of any shooting or major conflict to stabilize individuals and families, provide trauma counseling, and connect them to Family Success Planning coaches. Outreach Workers are often the first point of contact. They build the day-to-day trust that lets Credible Messengers intervene before a conflict turns violent.

### Why We Are the Right Partner

GOODProjects has served Southwest DC for nearly a decade. We have invested $8 million in Ward 6 families, earned recognition from Forbes, CBS 60 Minutes, NBC Nightly News, The New York Times, and The Washington Post, and built lasting partnerships with the DC Department of Employment Services, DC Parks and Recreation, and more than 20 community organizations. Washington Gas already serves the same Southwest DC blocks GOODZone patrols every day, so a safer corridor is a direct benefit to the customers, employees, and infrastructure Washington Gas maintains here.

### The Ask

We respectfully request $12,000 to fund two Community Outreach Workers for one year, extending GOODZone's daily street-level presence in Ward 6's highest-risk corridors and serving residents in and around Southwest DC's public housing communities.

## PART B: FULL APPLICATION

### Organization Description

GOODProjects is a Washington, DC nonprofit, founded in 2016, working to end the cycle of intergenerational poverty through a place-based, two-generation model. We pair children's education and youth development with the economic mobility, food security, health, and stability of the adults around them, so progress in one generation reinforces the next. We serve residents of James Creek, Syphax Gardens, and Greenleaf, three public housing communities in Southwest DC's Ward 6, where median household income is approximately $14,000 per year.

### Community Served

We serve residents of James Creek, Syphax Gardens, and Greenleaf, three public housing communities in Southwest DC's Ward 6. Approximately 95% of participants identify as Black or African American. Median household income is approximately $14,000 per year.

### Program Description

GOODZone is GOODProjects' violence interruption program, the third phase in a four-phase continuum that also includes Family Success Planning, GOODLearningHub afterschool programming, and JUMPSTART workforce development. GOODZone's field team, comprising Credible Messengers, Crisis Intervention Specialists, and Community Outreach Workers, covers Ward 6 corridors daily. Outreach Workers walk the same corridors every day, building the relationships that let the team identify conflicts before they escalate and connect at-risk residents to services. When a shooting or major conflict occurs, Crisis Intervention Specialists activate within 48 hours to stabilize affected individuals and families, provide trauma-informed counseling, and hand off to Family Success Planning coaches for longer-term support. This $12,000 request funds two additional Community Outreach Workers for one year of daily coverage.
