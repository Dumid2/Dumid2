   GOODProjects arts event; useful if photo/video documentation exists.
4. **STREAM programming** (the "Arts" component specifically): worth checking for any
   documented sessions.

**Action:** ask Adam Hickson (Communications Lead: he owns photo/video/social content) today
whether usable photo or video documentation exists from any of the above, dated within the last 3
years. If video, confirm it's not a highly-edited promotional piece (Addendum A explicitly says
not to submit those) and that it's under 5 minutes, or has a marked timecode if longer.

**Technical specs to hold every sample to, per Addendum A:**
- Photos: JPEG/JPG, minimum 72 dpi, max 8MB each
- Video: no highly-edited/promotional footage; no dim lighting, shaky video, or backlighting; if
  over 5 minutes, mark the exact timecode panelists should start at
- Audio: panelists cannot skip or rewind, so any clip must be pre-edited to the exact content
  wanted; if there's a visual element, submit as video instead
- Every sample must be labeled with a specific name and title so panelists know what they're
  looking at
- A website screenshot is not an adequate substitute for a real work sample; panelists do not
  click outside links
- Do not over-submit: Addendum A explicitly warns that adding more samples than needed weakens
  an application, not strengthens it. Pick a small number of the strongest, most relevant items.
