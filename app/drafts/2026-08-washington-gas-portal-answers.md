## Organization Capability

**Provide a brief overview, history, and mission of your organization.**

GOODProjects is a Washington, DC nonprofit founded in 2016, working to end the cycle of intergenerational poverty through a place based, two generation model. We pair children's education and youth development with the economic mobility, food security, health, and stability of the adults around them, so progress in one generation reinforces the next. We serve residents of James Creek, Syphax Gardens, and Greenleaf, three public housing communities in Southwest DC's Ward 6, where median household income is approximately $14,000 per year. Over ten years, GOODProjects has built an integrated continuum of care spanning family stabilization, afterschool education, violence interruption, and workforce development, and has invested more than $8 million in Ward 6 families. The organization has been recognized by Forbes, CBS 60 Minutes, and NBC Nightly News.

**How does your organization measure impact?**

GOODZone tracks outcomes across six domains: violence reduction, participant stabilization, crisis response, employment placement, program reach, and community perception. Violence reduction is measured against Metropolitan Police Department crime data for PSA 105, GOODZone's coverage area, which showed a 45% reduction in violent crime from 2023 to 2024 compared with a 12% increase citywide over the same period. Participant-level data, including crisis activations and referrals to Family Success Planning, is tracked through the organization's internal case management system and reported to funders on a mid-year and final basis, or per each funder's specific reporting schedule.

**Does your organization have any previous or currently pending legal actions against it?**

No.

**What are your long-term plans for this project?**

GOODZone's long-term goal is daily, fully staffed coverage across all five identified hotspot corridors in Ward 6 and Ward 8. This request moves toward that goal by adding two Community Outreach Workers for one year. GOODProjects will pursue continued and diversified funding, including renewed government support through ONSE and additional corporate and foundation partners, to make this expanded coverage permanent beyond the first year.

**Describe the criteria you will use to determine whether or not the project is successful.**

Success means sustained daily coverage across the funded corridors for the full grant term, continued or further reduction in violent incidents in MPD PSA 105 relative to the 2023 to 2024 baseline, and consistent referral of at-risk residents to Family Success Planning for longer-term stabilization.

**How will you measure success of your program/event? Please provide desired outputs and outcomes.**

Primary outcome: sustained reduction in violent crime in MPD PSA 105, GOODZone's coverage area, which fell from 53 incidents in 2023 to 29 in 2024. Secondary outcome: expanded daily outreach coverage across the five identified hotspot corridors. Outputs tracked include the number of daily outreach contacts, crisis activations within 48 hours of a shooting or major conflict, and referrals to Family Success Planning coaches. These are measured through MPD PSA 105 crime data, cross-referenced with GOODZone's internal six-domain evaluation framework.

**How are you promoting this program/event to the community?**

GOODZone's outreach model is built on daily, in-person presence rather than advertising. Credible Messengers and Outreach Workers walk the same corridors every day, building direct relationships with residents. GOODProjects also promotes the broader program through its partnerships with ONSE, the Metropolitan Police Department, and more than 20 community organizations, and through its own communications channels and community events.
