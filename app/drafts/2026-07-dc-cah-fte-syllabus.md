Step Into Heritage: A Dance and Humanities Field Trip Series
Syllabus (Pre-Trip, Post-Trip, and Staff PD Curriculum)

Instructional content for the CAH FY27 Field Trip Experiences series, designed by Stephanie Page-Baxter (Director of Youth Programming) with day-to-day delivery by Phileke Holland (Afterschool Lead). Audience: GOODLearningHub's 6th-8th grade cohort (30 students) attending Jefferson Middle School. Each session below connects to GOODLearningHub's existing Social-Emotional Learning (SEL) curriculum rather than introducing a new framework.

OPEN ITEM: this syllabus references general DC social studies and arts education standard areas, not specific OSSE standard codes. Citing exact codes would strengthen the application; Stephanie Page-Baxter is the right person to supply them if GOODLearningHub's curriculum is already mapped to specific codes. Nothing below invents a code number.

## Trip 1: Arena Stage, Step Afrika!'s Magical Musical Holiday Step Show

### Session 1: Pre-Trip Workshop (classroom, the week before the trip)

**Learning objective:** students can describe the history and cultural significance of stepping as a living African American tradition, and name at least one way it connects to their own community or identity.

**Standard area (general, pending exact code from Stephanie Page-Baxter):** DC arts education standards for dance, grades 6-8, connecting movement and performance to cultural context.

**Activities:**
1. Opening discussion: what do students already know about stepping? (10 minutes)
2. Direct instruction: the origins of stepping in Black fraternity and sorority life at Historically Black Colleges and Universities (HBCUs), and its roots in African diasporic rhythm and movement traditions (15 minutes)
3. Short video or audio example of stepping, followed by guided discussion connecting the tradition to the SEL themes GOODLearningHub already teaches: identity, community, and collective voice (15 minutes)
4. Preview of the performance: what to expect at Arena Stage, including the Interactive add-on's teaching-artist component (10 minutes)
5. Closing: students write or share one question they want answered by the performance (10 minutes)

**Materials:** discussion guide (GOODLearningHub-developed), video or audio example, Arena Stage's own pre-show materials if provided in advance.

### Session 2: Field Trip Day

Matinee performance of Step Afrika!'s Magical Musical Holiday Step Show, plus the Arena Stage Interactive add-on (workshop or backstage tour led by Arena Stage teaching artists, pre-show program, printed Learning Guides). No separate lesson plan; Arena Stage's teaching artists lead the on-site instructional component.

### Session 3: Post-Trip Reflection (classroom, within 1 week after the trip)

**Learning objective:** students can articulate a personal or community connection to the performance, in writing or verbally, as measured by the post-trip survey (series-wide target: 75%).

**Activities:**
1. Open discussion: what stood out about the performance? (10 minutes)
2. Guided reflection prompt: "How does stepping's history of community and collective voice connect to your own life or community?" Students respond in writing or verbally, based on individual comfort and need. (20 minutes)
3. Small-group sharing (15 minutes)
4. Post-trip survey administered (10 minutes)

**Materials:** reflection prompt handout, post-trip survey (GOODLearningHub's standard evaluation tool).

## Trip 2: Ford's Theatre, Free Field Trip Program (Historic Site Visit)

### Session 1: Pre-Trip Workshop (classroom, the week before the trip)

**Learning objective:** students can describe the basic history of Lincoln's presidency, the Civil War, and the events at Ford's Theatre, and connect the theme of leadership to their own understanding of community leadership.

**Standard area (general, pending exact code from Stephanie Page-Baxter):** DC social studies standards for grade 7-8 U.S. history, covering the Civil War era and its aftermath.

**Activities:**
1. Opening discussion: what do students already know about Abraham Lincoln and the Civil War? (10 minutes)
2. Direct instruction: the basic timeline of the Civil War, Lincoln's presidency, and the assassination at Ford's Theatre, using age-appropriate primary-source excerpts (15 minutes)
3. Guided discussion connecting the theme of leadership and legacy to the SEL themes GOODLearningHub already teaches: identity, community, and collective voice, reframed here as leadership and impact (15 minutes)
4. Preview of the site visit: what to expect at the Ford's Theatre Museum, the theatre itself, and the Petersen House (10 minutes)
5. Closing: students write or share one question they want answered by the visit (10 minutes)

**Materials:** discussion guide (GOODLearningHub-developed), age-appropriate primary-source excerpts, Ford's Theatre's own pre-visit materials if provided in advance.

### Session 2: Field Trip Day

Guided visit through the Ford's Theatre Museum, the theatre and President's Box, and the Petersen House and Aftermath Exhibits, led by Ford's Theatre's education staff. No separate lesson plan; Ford's Theatre's own staff lead the on-site instructional component.

### Session 3: Post-Trip Reflection (classroom, within 1 week after the trip)

**Learning objective:** students can articulate a personal or community connection to what they learned, in writing or verbally, as measured by the post-trip survey (series-wide target: 75%).

**Activities:**
1. Open discussion: what stood out about the visit? (10 minutes)
2. Guided reflection prompt: "What does leadership mean to you, and how does Lincoln's story connect to leaders in your own community?" Students respond in writing or verbally, based on individual comfort and need. (20 minutes)
3. Small-group sharing (15 minutes)
4. Post-trip survey administered (10 minutes)

**Materials:** reflection prompt handout, post-trip survey (GOODLearningHub's standard evaluation tool).

## Staff Professional Development Session

**Audience:** GOODLearningHub's Youth Services Advocates.

**Learning objective:** staff can identify at least one way to integrate dance-/movement-based learning and one way to integrate historical-inquiry-based learning into ongoing afterschool programming, beyond this series' two field trips.

**Proposed agenda (60-90 minutes, exact length to be confirmed by Stephanie Page-Baxter):**
1. Recap of Trip 1 and Trip 2 outcomes, including student reflection themes (15 minutes)
2. Facilitated discussion: what worked in the pre/post-trip workshop model, and where could it extend to other GOODLearningHub programming (SEL sessions, STREAM programming, GOODSports)? (20 minutes)
3. Practical strategies for movement-based learning (drawing on Trip 1) (15 minutes)
4. Practical strategies for historical-inquiry-based learning (drawing on Trip 2) (15 minutes)
5. Closing: each Youth Services Advocate commits to one strategy to try in their own group (10 minutes)

**Owner:** Stephanie Page-Baxter, with input from Arena Stage's and Ford's Theatre's education staff where possible (OPEN ITEM: confirm whether either partner can send a representative or provide materials for this session).
