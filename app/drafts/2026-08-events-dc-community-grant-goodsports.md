## SCOPE OF WORK / PROJECT NARRATIVE

### Program Purpose and Goals

GOODProjects requests $18,000 to deepen GOODSports, the sports and life-skills intervention track within GOODLearningHub, serving Black youth ages 5 to 14 in Southwest DC's Ward 6 public housing communities. GOODSports uses football, basketball, and dance as vehicles for teamwork, discipline, goal-setting, conflict resolution, and emotional regulation, directly matching Events DC's Community Grant Program's focus on youth sports organizations in the District.

### Target Population

Residents of James Creek, Syphax Gardens, and Greenleaf public housing communities in Southwest DC's Ward 6.

| Total Unduplicated Participants | 150 in summer (GOODCamp) plus 50 to 100 in afterschool (year-round) |
|---|---|
| Age Range | 5 to 14 |
| Primary Demographic | Black / African American (100% of GOODSports participants) |
| Income Level | 100% below the federal poverty line |
| Geographic Area | Ward 6, Southwest DC (King Greenleaf Recreation Center and DCHA Family Enhancement Center) |

### Activities and Deliverables

| Deliverable | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Daily football, basketball, and dance sessions for 150 youth (GOODCamp) | 8 weeks, summer 2026 |
| Weekly football, basketball, and dance sessions for 50 to 100 youth (afterschool) | 30+ weeks, school year |
| Weekly 20-minute Life Skills Huddle (conflict resolution, goal-setting, emotional regulation, leadership) | Concurrent with all sessions |
| Trauma-informed sports facilitation training for coaches and Youth Services Advocates | Start of program year |
| Final Report | 30 days post-program-year |
