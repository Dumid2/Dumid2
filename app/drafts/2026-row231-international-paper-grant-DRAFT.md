## PART A — LETTER OF INTENT (LOI)

### Opening Paragraph — Mission and Ask

GOODProjects operates GOODLearningHub, a year-round academic and nutrition program serving 400+ youth daily in Southwest DC. We respectfully request $20,900 from the International Paper Foundation to fund tutoring materials, nutrition support, and Youth Services Advocate time for the 2026-2027 program year.

### The Problem We Are Solving

Youth in Southwest DC's Ward 6 public housing communities face compounding barriers to academic growth: under-resourced schools, food insecurity, and limited access to structured, high-dosage academic support outside the school day. In FY25, GOODLearningHub answered this need directly, delivering 264 tutoring sessions and 11,220 meals to youth ages 5 to 14, but sustaining that dual level of academic and nutrition support each year requires dedicated funding for both tracks together, not one in isolation.

### Our Approach

GOODLearningHub operates daily at King Greenleaf Recreation Center, combining high-dosage 1:1 and small-group tutoring on the IXL platform with daily nutritious meals, Youth Services Advocates assigned to each student, and social-emotional learning through peace circles and mindfulness. In FY25, students answered 174,653 academic questions and mastered 1,912 skills across core subjects, while the program's partnership with DC Central Kitchen kept meals flowing alongside the academic work.

### Why We Are the Right Partner

GOODProjects has served Southwest DC for nearly a decade. We have invested $8 million in Ward 6 families, earned recognition from Forbes, CBS 60 Minutes, and NBC Nightly News, and built lasting partnerships with the DC Department of Employment Services, DC Parks and Recreation, and more than 20 community organizations.

### The Ask

We respectfully request $20,900 to fund tutoring supplies, nutrition support, and Youth Services Advocate time for GOODLearningHub's 2026-2027 program year, sustaining both the academic and hunger-relief components of the program together.

## PART B — FULL APPLICATION

### Organization Description

GOODProjects is a Washington, DC nonprofit (founded 2016) working to end the cycle of intergenerational poverty through a place-based, two-generation model, pairing children's education and youth development with the economic mobility, food security, health, and stability of the adults around them, so that progress in one generation reinforces the next. We serve residents of James Creek, Syphax Gardens, and Greenleaf, three public housing communities in Southwest DC's Ward 6, where the median household income is approximately $14,000.

### Community Served

We serve residents of James Creek, Syphax Gardens, and Greenleaf, three public housing communities in Southwest DC's Ward 6. Approximately 95% of participants identify as Black or African American. Median household income is approximately $14,000 per year.

### Program Description

GOODLearningHub is the second phase of GOODProjects' continuum of care, the youth-facing anchor operating year-round at King Greenleaf Recreation Center. It serves youth ages 5 to 14 through high-dosage tutoring, daily meals, Youth Services Advocates, social-emotional learning, and summer enrichment through GOODCamps. Children of Family Success Planning-enrolled families are automatically connected, linking the academic and family-stabilization tracks of GOODProjects' continuum. This $20,900 request funds tutoring materials, nutrition support delivered in partnership with DC Central Kitchen, and Youth Services Advocate time for the 2026-2027 program year.
