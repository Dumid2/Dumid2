## WHAT WE ARE PROPOSING

A weekday matinee field trip for 30 GOODLearningHub youth to see **Step Afrika!'s Magical
Musical Holiday Step Show** at **Arena Stage** (1101 6th Street SW, Washington, DC), paired with
a pre-trip classroom workshop, an on-site workshop/backstage tour with Arena Stage teaching
artists, and a staff professional development session.

**Why this show, specifically:**
- It is Arena Stage's own production, in DC — satisfies CAH's hard requirement that all FTE
  programming take place within the District (deviations disqualify the application)
- It is a family-friendly holiday production built around African American stepping — fits our
  ~95% Black/African American youth population and ties naturally to an SEL discussion
- It runs December 4–23, 2026, which lands inside GOODLearningHub's existing Fall II session
  (Nov 30 – Dec 17, 2026) — no new session or special scheduling required
- We checked: none of GOODProjects' existing 2026 field trips (Wolf Trap, AnnMarie Sculpture
  Garden, Creative Cauldron, etc.) are DC-based, so none of them could have been used for this
  grant. This is a new trip built specifically to meet CAH's DC-only rule.

**Proposed date: Thursday, December 10, 2026** (weekday matinee).
Arena Stage hasn't published its 2026/27 matinee calendar yet. This date is a projection based on
last year's run, which held weekday matinees on Thursdays at 12PM (Dec 11 and Dec 18, 2025).
Backup: Thursday, December 17, 2026 — but that's the last day of the Fall II session, so it has
no buffer if anything shifts. Once you approve the concept, the next step is calling Arena Stage
Group Sales (202-488-4380 / groups@arenastage.org) to lock the real date.

## WHO GOES

30 of GOODLearningHub's 73 enrolled students, ages 5–14, from James Creek, Syphax Gardens, and
Greenleaf public housing (Ward 6). Below 200% federal poverty line.

call on whether to (a) narrow the cohort to one band — elementary-age fits this show best — or
(b) confirm directly with CAH that a mixed afterschool cohort is acceptable. This hasn't been
checked against the actual FY27 RFA text yet.
