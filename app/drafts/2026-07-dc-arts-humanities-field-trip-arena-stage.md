## SCOPE OF WORK / PROJECT NARRATIVE

### Program Purpose and Goals

GOODProjects requests $4,500 to provide a GOODLearningHub arts and humanities field trip experience: a weekday student matinee at Arena Stage, a professional theater in Southwest DC, for youth enrolled in GOODLearningHub and GOODCamp. This directly matches CAH's Field Trip Experiences goal of providing comprehensive arts and humanities field trip experiences that advance student achievement, paired with pre- and post-trip workshops and professional development for staff.

### Target Population

Residents of James Creek, Syphax Gardens, and Greenleaf public housing communities in Southwest DC's Ward 6, enrolled in GOODLearningHub.

| Total Unduplicated Participants | 30 youth per matinee session (Arena Stage's D.C. Ticket Partnership group size), drawn from GOODLearningHub's 73 enrolled students |
|---|---|
| Age Range | 5 to 14 |
| Primary Demographic | Black / African American (approximately 95%) |
| Income Level | Below 200% federal poverty line |
| Geographic Area | Ward 6, Southwest DC; field trip destination is Arena Stage, 1101 6th Street SW, Washington, DC 20024, itself in Southwest DC near GOODProjects' own service area |

### Activities and Deliverables

| Deliverable | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Select production and book a weekday student matinee through Arena Stage's D.C. Ticket Partnership or Group Sales | Month 1 |
| Pre-trip in-class workshop connecting the production's themes to GOODLearningHub's Social-Emotional Learning curriculum | Month 2, before the matinee |
| Field trip: matinee performance for 30 youth, including transportation | Month 2 |
| Post-show talkback with Arena Stage teaching artists | Same day as matinee |
| Professional development session for Youth Services Advocates on integrating theater-based learning into afterschool programming | Month 2 or 3 |
| Final Report | 30 days post-program |
