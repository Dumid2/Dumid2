## Row 200 — Corning Incorporated Foundation: likely NO-GO

Corning's Community Impact grants are restricted to organizations within
a 20-mile radius of a Corning business location with 50+ employees.
Corning's major U.S. sites are in upstate New York and other
manufacturing/tech hub locations; DC is not a known Corning site.
Applications are also by invitation only: eligible organizations email
philanthropy@corning.com to introduce themselves, and Corning decides
whether to invite a full application. There is no evidence GOODProjects
qualifies geographically.

**Recommendation:** skip, unless Neo knows of a specific Corning DC-area
facility or an existing relationship. If pursued, the first step is an
introductory email, not a proposal draft.

## Row 218 — HRSA Public Health Training Centers Program: likely NO-GO (entity type mismatch)

This federal opportunity (grants.gov listing 362379, deadline July 17,
2026) funds two things: Regional Public Health Training Centers
(leadership institutes for the public health workforce, typically run by
schools of public health or academic public health training centers) and
a Community Health Worker Training Program expanding the CHW workforce
nationally. Both components appear built for institutions with public
health academic training capacity, which GOODProjects does not have.
This looks like a poor entity-type fit, similar in kind to the HHS Youth
Homelessness eligibility conflict already on hold (row 191), not a
geography problem this time but a capacity/mission mismatch.

**Recommendation:** skip unless Neo has a specific reason to believe
GOODProjects qualifies as a public health workforce training entity.
Given the 7-day-out deadline, this needs a fast decision if it is to be
pursued at all.

## Row 138 — USDA CACFP: different track, not a written proposal

CACFP (Child and Adult Care Food Program) is a federal meal-reimbursement
program administered in DC by OSSE (the state agency), not a competitive
narrative grant. Organizations become a CACFP sponsor through an
administrative application and ongoing compliance process with OSSE,
not a proposal with an ask amount and narrative sections. No deadline is
listed on this row, consistent with it being a rolling
enrollment/sponsorship process rather than a grant cycle.

**Recommendation:** this needs Cole or a program-operations lead to
handle as an OSSE sponsor enrollment, not a grant draft from Dumi. Worth
confirming with Neo whether GOODProjects is already a CACFP sponsor via
existing afterschool meal programming (DC Central Kitchen partnership
suggests meals are already happening, possibly already through CACFP).
