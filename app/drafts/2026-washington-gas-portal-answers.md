## ORGANIZATION NARRATIVE

**Provide a brief overview, history, and mission of your organization. (under 250 words)**
GOODProjects is a Washington, DC nonprofit, founded in 2016, working to end the cycle of intergenerational poverty through a place-based, two-generation model. We pair children's education and youth development with the economic mobility, food security, health, and stability of the adults around them, so progress in one generation reinforces the next. We serve residents of James Creek, Syphax Gardens, and Greenleaf, three public housing communities in Southwest DC's Ward 6, where median household income is approximately $14,000 per year. Our work spans a four-phase continuum: Family Success Planning for parents and caregivers, GOODLearningHub afterschool programming for children ages 5 to 14, GOODZone violence interruption for residents ages 18 to 35, and JUMPSTART workforce development for youth and young adults. Since 2016, GOODProjects has invested $8 million in Ward 6 families and earned recognition from Forbes, CBS 60 Minutes, and NBC Nightly News. We have built lasting partnerships with the DC Department of Employment Services, DC Parks and Recreation, and more than 20 community organizations. GOODZone, the program at the center of this request, has helped drive a 45 percent reduction in violent crime in its coverage area over the past two years, compared to a 12 percent increase citywide over the same period. Our mission is to give every family in our community the tools and support to build lasting stability, opportunity, and safety, one generation at a time.

## PROJECT DETAILS

**What is the name of this project?**

**Provide a brief description of the request. (under 250 words)**
GOODProjects respectfully requests $13,200 from Washington Gas's Community Investment Program to fund two additional Community Outreach Workers for one year, extending GOODZone's daily street-level presence in Ward 6's highest-risk corridors. GOODZone is GOODProjects' violence interruption program, deploying Credible Messengers, Outreach Workers, and Crisis Intervention Specialists, all with direct lived experience of violence and incarceration, across five identified hotspot corridors: M Street SE, Buzzard Point, Syphax Gardens, Greenleaf Gardens, and the Waterfront Metro area. Outreach Workers build the daily trust that lets the team mediate conflicts before they turn violent, and the team activates within 48 hours of any shooting or major conflict to stabilize affected individuals and families. Over the past six years, GOODZone's coverage area recorded 249 violent incidents, compared with 601 in a neighboring area, despite serving a lower-income population. Violent crime in the GOODZone fell from 53 incidents in 2023 to 29 in 2024, a 45 percent reduction, compared to a 12 percent increase in violent crime citywide over the same period. Sustaining that decline requires continued street-level presence, and current funding covers a smaller outreach team than the corridors require. This request adds incremental daily coverage on top of GOODZone's existing, funded programming.
