# GOODProjects Grant Qualification Note
Row 201, 2026 dev bible tab: Carlson Family Foundation

## Decision: NO-GO (flagged, not drafted)

## Why

The Carlson Family Foundation funds organizations serving the **Twin
Cities metro area (Minneapolis and St. Paul)**. GOODProjects' service
area is Southwest DC's Ward 6 public housing communities, well outside
the Foundation's stated geographic scope. This is a hard geographic
disqualifier independent of program fit, similar to the Capitol Hill
Community Foundation and Entergy issues already flagged on other rows.

There is a second, independent blocker: the Foundation has stated it
**will not be accepting Letters of Inquiry (LOIs) for 2026 funding
requests**, as it is focused on deepening collaboration with current
grantees rather than intake from new organizations. Even a Minneapolis
or St. Paul-based organization would not be able to apply this cycle.

## What was found

- **Geographic focus:** Twin Cities metro (Minneapolis/St. Paul) only.
- **Eligibility:** 501(c)(3) tax-exempt nonprofits (or fiscal-sponsored
  equivalents for the Youth Participatory Grantmaking Program).
- **Priority areas:** the Constellation Initiative (youth, with an
  emphasis on youth of color, in Minneapolis/St. Paul), Vibrant
  Communities (inclusive access to arts, culture, and green spaces),
  and Sex Trafficking Prevention.
- **2026 status:** not accepting LOIs for 2026, as the Foundation is
  prioritizing existing grantee relationships.

Source: [Carlson Family Foundation — Grantmaking](https://carlsonfamilyfoundation.org/grantmaking/),
[Carlson Family Foundation — Important Notice](https://carlsonfamilyfoundation.org/)

## Recommendation

Skip. Both the geography (Twin Cities only, not DC) and the 2026 intake
pause (no LOIs being accepted) independently rule this out. No narrative
quality would overcome either gate. Revisit only if GOODProjects
develops Twin Cities-based programming, which is outside its current
mission and service area.
