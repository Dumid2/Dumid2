# GOODProjects Grant Qualification Note
Row 231, 2026 dev bible tab: International Paper Grant

## Decision: NO-GO (flagged, not drafted)

## Why

International Paper's own published guideline is unambiguous: **"If your
organization does not have an International Paper location in the
community, you are not eligible for grant funding."** Contributions are
considered only in communities where IP has a significant employee
presence, within a locally determined radius of a facility. IP's U.S.
grant activity is concentrated in mill and plant towns (e.g.,
Columbus/Brunswick, NC and similar facility communities), not urban
centers without an IP site. Washington, DC has no IP facility, so
GOODProjects fails the foundation's threshold eligibility rule before
any programmatic fit is considered.

Note: an earlier triage pass on this same opportunity is recorded in
`2026-batch-triage-notes.md` under row 229 in the dev bible tab at that
time, flagged urgent with a due date of July 13. The row number appears
to have shifted to 231 in a later revision of the tracker; row 231 in
that earlier pass referred to a separate, already-drafted TD Bank
sponsorship request. The underlying IP eligibility issue is unchanged
regardless of row numbering.

## What was found

- **Geographic/facility rule:** grants limited to communities with an
  active IP facility and employee presence; no IP location, no
  eligibility.
- **Entity eligibility:** 501(c)(3) or 509(a)(1)/(2)/(3), or a
  municipal/school/fire-district entity with a fiscal conduit letter.
- **Signature causes:** education, hunger, health and wellness, disaster
  relief, and environmental initiatives, with priority given to requests
  showing active IP employee engagement, which GOODProjects has no basis
  to claim without a local facility.
- **Financial standards:** requires evidence of competent management,
  good financial/public standing, and low administrative/fundraising
  expense ratios.
- **Required documentation** if the facility gate were met: W-9, audited
  financials, itemized program budget, current funder list, board list.

## Recommendation

Skip. Confirm with Neo whether GOODProjects has any confirmed IP
facility connection near DC before spending further prep time; absent
one, this fails eligibility screening regardless of how strong the
narrative is, and the required documentation burden (W-9, audited
financials, full funder and board lists) is not worth investing against
a threshold disqualifier.
