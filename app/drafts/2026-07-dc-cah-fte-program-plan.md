## 5. Staff Professional Development Session (Target: between Trip 1 and Trip 2, or immediately after Trip 2)

- Audience: GOODLearningHub's Youth Services Advocates.
- Content: integrating both dance-/movement-based learning (informed by Trip 1) and historical-inquiry-based learning (informed by Trip 2) into ongoing afterschool programming.
- Owner: Stephanie Page-Baxter, with input from Arena Stage's and Ford's Theatre's education staff where possible.
- Budget: covered within the "staff planning and coordination time" line, not a separately ticketed session; confirm with Cole Bradshaw whether an outside facilitator fee is ever anticipated (none is currently budgeted).

## 6. Evaluation and Reporting

1. Attendance tracking: GOODLearningHub's standard tools, per trip and per workshop.
2. Post-trip surveys: administered after each trip's reflection session; target 75% of students able to connect the trip's themes to a personal or SEL discussion topic, per trip.
3. Qualitative reflections: collected in writing or verbally during each post-trip session.
4. Staff PD completion: tracked against the full Youth Services Advocate roster; target 100%.
5. Melissa Wippenaar-Page compiles the Final Report, due October 15, 2027 (30 days after Trip 2, consistent with CAH's reporting requirement for awards under $12,500).
