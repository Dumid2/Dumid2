**9. In more detail, please provide an overview of the proposed program.**
*(Maximum 1000 characters)*

GOODZone is GOODProjects' violence interruption program serving public housing communities in Ward 6 and Ward 8, anchored at King Greenleaf Recreation Center. The program deploys trained credible messengers: community members with lived experience of violence who hold the trust needed to de-escalate conflict before it turns lethal, mediate disputes, and connect high-risk individuals to services. GOODZone operates across three areas: (1) Conflict mediation and violence interruption. Interrupters maintain active relationships with the highest-risk residents and respond in real time to emerging tensions. (2) Trauma-informed grief support. Residents who have lost someone to gun violence receive structured support and referrals to licensed mental health providers. (3) Pathway navigation. Participants connect to JUMPSTART for workforce development and the Family Support Program for wraparound coaching, creating exits from the conditions that drive violence.

**10. List at least three tangible goals of the proposed program, along with the individuals or teams responsible for achieving them.**
*(Maximum 1000 characters)*

Goal 1: By September 30, 2027, GOODZone violence interrupters will conduct at least 50 documented conflict mediations, with at least 85% of them not escalating into violence. Responsible: GOODZone Program Lead and violence interruption staff. Goal 2: By September 30, 2027, GOODZone credible messengers will engage at least 75 unduplicated Ward 6 and Ward 8 residents at elevated risk through direct outreach, with engagement tracked in the case management system. Responsible: Credible messenger outreach team. Goal 3: By September 30, 2027, a minimum of 30 GOODZone participants will be connected to at least one wraparound service: workforce development, housing stability, mental health, or education. Responsible: GOODZone Program Lead in coordination with Family Support Program staff.

*(881 characters)*

**11. How will your organization measure and evaluate the success of the program's goals and outcomes?**
*(Maximum 1000 characters)*

GOODProjects measures GOODZone outcomes at three levels. Data collection: each violence interrupter records every conflict mediation, outreach contact, grief support session, and referral in the organization's case management system, with each entry dated and linked to a participant record. Data review: GOODZone leadership reviews each interrupter's field reports weekly and compiles monthly analyses of mediated conflicts, which are shared with the community and neighborhood leaders. Success is measured by reduced violent crime, de-escalated conflicts, violence interrupters hired through this grant, participants who move into stable employment with other Ward 6 employers, and observed behavior change at the individual and community level.

**12. The target population for these funds includes individuals identified as being at increased risk for violence and opioid-related overdose. Please identify which population within this group your proposed program will serve.**
*(Maximum 500 characters)*

Individuals aged 16 to 25 living in or adjacent to public housing and low-income housing units within Ward 6, PSA 105, identified by community elders or school systems as high-risk for gang or crew affiliation. Rationale: rapid gentrification in Ward 6 has created stark socioeconomic divides. Targeting youth at these geographic boundaries addresses the localized turf conflicts and economic desperation that drive both community violence and entry into illicit drug networks.
