# GOODProjects Grant Qualification Note
Row 202, 2026 dev bible tab: Corning Incorporated Community Impact and Investment

## Decision: NO-GO (flagged, not drafted)

## Why

Corning's Community Impact and Investment grants are restricted to
community-based programs within a **20-mile radius of a Corning business
location with at least 50 employees**. Corning's major U.S. sites are
concentrated in upstate New York (Corning/Elmira) and other
manufacturing/tech hub locations. Washington, DC is not a known Corning
facility community, and there is no evidence GOODProjects falls within
the required radius of any qualifying site.

On top of the geographic gate, the program is **invitation-only**:
eligible organizations first introduce themselves by emailing
philanthropy@corning.com, and Corning decides whether to invite a full
application. Corning does not accept unsolicited proposals. There is no
record of GOODProjects having received such an invitation.

Note: an earlier triage pass on this same opportunity is recorded in
`2026-batch-triage-notes.md` under row 200 in the dev bible tab at that
time; the row number appears to have shifted to 202 in a later revision
of the tracker. The underlying funder and eligibility issue are
unchanged.

## What was found

- **Focus areas:** arts and culture, STEM education, small business
  development, workforce support, and local DEI efforts.
- **Geographic requirement:** within 20 miles of a Corning site with
  50+ employees; the Corning/Elmira, NY window runs February 15 to
  September 15 for a rolling annual cycle.
- **Eligibility:** 501(c)(3) or 509(a) public charity, no written
  discrimination policy.
- **Not funded:** event sponsorships, conferences, individuals,
  religious or political causes.
- **Entry point:** an introductory email to philanthropy@corning.com,
  not a direct application.

## Recommendation

Skip, unless Neo knows of a specific Corning facility near DC or an
existing relationship with the philanthropy team that would support an
invitation. If Neo wants to pursue it anyway, the correct next step is
the introductory email to philanthropy@corning.com, not a proposal
draft, since Corning will not accept an uninvited application regardless
of narrative quality.
