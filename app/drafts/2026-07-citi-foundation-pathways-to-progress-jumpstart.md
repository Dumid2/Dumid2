## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Citi Foundation's Pathways to Progress initiative invests in young people aged 16 to 24 who need leadership training, real work experience, and professional networks to move into employment or start a business. GOODProjects requests $125,000 to run JUMPSTART for 35 GOODLearningHub alumni over 12 months: career readiness training, a six-week paid internship with a GOODZone business partner, and 90 days of retention support after hire. We are asking Citi to fund the next cohort moving through a pathway that has already placed youth in paid roles with zero incidents of violence or program disruption.

## ORGANIZATIONAL OVERVIEW

GOODProjects is a Washington, DC nonprofit, founded in 2016, working to end the cycle of intergenerational poverty through a place-based, two-generation model that pairs children's education and youth development with the economic mobility, food security, health, and stability of the adults around them. We serve residents of James Creek, Syphax Gardens, and Greenleaf, three public housing communities in Southwest DC's Ward 6, where the median household income is approximately $14,000.

GOODProjects operates with a staff of 30 (11 full-time, 19 part-time) and 50 volunteers, and has invested more than $8 million in Southwest DC since 2016. CEO Darius Baxter (2021 Forbes 30 Under 30, 2023 Washingtonian of the Year) built the organization from a five-week summer camp in 2015 into a full continuum of care. Our 2024 audit by Desai Company returned an unmodified opinion, signed September 7, 2025.

## COMMUNITY NEED

Ward 6 public housing communities face an unemployment and underemployment problem that starts young. Southwest DC youth grow up with limited exposure to workplaces, few professional networks, and few paid opportunities to build a resume before adulthood. Without an intentional bridge, that gap becomes a lifelong barrier to economic mobility, the same barrier GOODProjects' co-founders set out to break in 2016.

GOODProjects already has that bridge built. GOODLearningHub has run continuously in Ward 6 for ten years, and JUMPSTART converts that investment into paid work: 50 or more youth have moved through paid or volunteer mentorship roles since 2021, and more than 100 alumni have engaged with zero incidents of violence or program disruption. What GOODProjects needs now is funding to grow the number of youth who move through that pipeline each year, exactly the gap Citi Foundation's Youth Economic Opportunity program exists to close.

## PROJECT DESCRIPTION

### Program Anchor
JUMPSTART / Youth Workforce Development Initiative, the fourth stage of GOODProjects' continuum of care. JUMPSTART pulls GOODLearningHub alumni back as mentors, interns, and employees, and connects them to GOODZone business partners ready to hire.

### Target Population
GOODLearningHub alumni ages 14 to 24, living in Ward 6, with an active Family Success Planning family connection. This cohort matches Citi Foundation's stated Pathways to Progress focus on youth primarily aged 16 to 24 who need skills training, experiential learning, and access to professional networks to become employed or start a business.

### Activities and Approach
This grant funds one full cohort of 35 youth through JUMPSTART's four-stage pathway:

1. **Alumni Tracking & Intake (Weeks 1-2):** proactive outreach, intake assessment, and a dedicated coach assigned to each participant.
2. **Career Launchpad Training (Weeks 3-4):** a 40-hour intensive covering resume building, interview preparation, professional communication, financial literacy, social-emotional learning, and workplace etiquette.
3. **Paid Internship Placement (Weeks 5-10):** direct placement with a GOODZone business partner at $15 per hour, 20 hours per week, for six weeks, with weekly trauma-informed wellness check-ins through C3 Wellness.
4. **Placement & 90-Day Retention (Weeks 11-24):** a dedicated placement coordinator supports the transition from internship to permanent hire, with retention tracked through 90 days.

Young people are not passive recipients of this design. Each cohort's Career Launchpad curriculum is reviewed with outgoing JUMPSTART alumni before the next cohort starts, so participants who went through the pathway shape what the next group is taught.

## EQUITY STATEMENT

We operate on the principle that economic mobility requires addressing both skills gaps and systemic barriers at the same time. We practice equity by removing barriers rather than asking young people to overcome them on their own. Our hiring, programming, and partnerships prioritize people with lived experience of the challenges we address, and JUMPSTART itself is built on that logic: it hires its own graduates as mentors and staff. We design this program with the young people it serves, not for them.

## SUSTAINABILITY

GOODProjects does not depend on single-source funding. FY25 revenue included $985,500 in foundation and trust grants, $384,403 in local government grants, and $180,000 in individual and business contributions. JUMPSTART itself is designed to be self-reinforcing: SWBID places five youth annually into paid stipend positions within GOODProjects, GOODZone business partners have an ongoing hiring pipeline built into the program design, and graduates return as paid mentors for the next cohort. A Citi Foundation grant funds one cohort's direct costs; the placement relationships and staff capacity it builds carry into future cohorts regardless of this grant's term.
