## PART A — LETTER OF INTENT (LOI)

### Opening Paragraph — Mission and Ask

GOODProjects operates JUMPSTART, a four-stage workforce pathway that moves Southwest DC youth from paid internship to permanent employment. We respectfully request $27,060 from Corning's Community Impact and Investment program to fund 12 paid internship placements for JUMPSTART participants during the 2026-2027 program year.

### The Problem We Are Solving

Young adults in Southwest DC's Ward 6 public housing communities face a workforce on-ramp problem: strong afterschool investment through childhood, then a gap in structured pathways to paid work once they age out of youth programming. Without a bridge, alumni of years of tutoring and mentorship risk idling in a labor market that rarely reaches into James Creek, Syphax Gardens, and Greenleaf. GOODProjects has already built the relationships and the trust; what participants need next is a paid, supervised first step into the workforce.

### Our Approach

JUMPSTART converts nine years of investment in GOODLearningHub youth into permanent employment through a four-stage pathway: alumni tracking and intake, a 40-hour Career Launchpad training in resume writing, interviewing, and workplace communication, a six-week paid internship with a GOODZone Business Partner at $15 per hour, and a 90-day retention period supported by a dedicated Placement Coordinator. Each stage builds on the last, so a young person who starts in outreach can finish in a permanent job.

### Why We Are the Right Partner

GOODProjects has served Southwest DC for nearly a decade. We have invested $8 million in Ward 6 families, earned recognition from Forbes, CBS 60 Minutes, and NBC Nightly News, and built lasting partnerships with the DC Department of Employment Services, DC Parks and Recreation, and more than 20 community organizations.

### The Ask

We respectfully request $27,060 to fund 12 paid internship placements through JUMPSTART's Stage 3, connecting Southwest DC young adults to GOODZone Business Partners for six weeks of supervised, paid work experience.

## PART B — FULL APPLICATION

### Organization Description

GOODProjects is a Washington, DC nonprofit (founded 2016) working to end the cycle of intergenerational poverty through a place-based, two-generation model, pairing children's education and youth development with the economic mobility, food security, health, and stability of the adults around them, so that progress in one generation reinforces the next. We serve residents of James Creek, Syphax Gardens, and Greenleaf, three public housing communities in Southwest DC's Ward 6, where the median household income is approximately $14,000.

### Community Served

We serve residents of James Creek, Syphax Gardens, and Greenleaf, three public housing communities in Southwest DC's Ward 6. Approximately 95% of participants identify as Black or African American. Median household income is approximately $14,000 per year.

### Program Description

JUMPSTART is the fourth phase of GOODProjects' continuum of care, following Family Success Planning, GOODLearningHub afterschool programming, and GOODZone violence interruption. It serves GOODLearningHub alumni ages 14 to 24 who carry an active Family Success Planning family connection. Participants move through four stages: proactive outreach and intake with a dedicated coach, a 40-hour Career Launchpad covering resume building, interview practice, professional communication, financial literacy, and workplace etiquette, a six-week paid internship placement with a GOODZone Business Partner at $15 per hour for 20 hours per week, and a 90-day retention period with a dedicated Placement Coordinator managing the transition from internship to permanent hire. This $27,060 request funds Stage 3 internship placements for 12 participants during the 2026-2027 program year.
