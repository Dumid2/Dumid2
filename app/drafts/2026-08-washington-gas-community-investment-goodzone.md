## Organization Capability

**Provide a brief overview, history, and mission of your organization.**

GOODProjects is a Washington, DC, nonprofit founded in 2016 that works to end the cycle of intergenerational poverty through a place-based, two-generation model. We pair children's education and youth development with the economic mobility, food security, health, and stability of the adults around them, so that progress in one generation reinforces progress in the next. We serve residents of James Creek, Syphax Gardens, and Greenleaf, three public housing communities in Southwest DC's Ward 6, where median household income is approximately $14,000 per year. Over ten years, GOODProjects has built an integrated continuum of care spanning family stabilization, afterschool education, violence interruption, and workforce development, and has invested more than $8 million in Ward 6 families. The organization has been recognized by Forbes, CBS 60 Minutes, NBC Nightly News, The New York Times, and The Washington Post.

**How does your organization measure impact?**

GOODZone tracks outcomes across six domains: violence reduction, participant stabilization, crisis response, employment placement, program reach, and community perception. Violence reduction is measured against Metropolitan Police Department crime data for PSA 105, GOODZone's coverage area, which showed a 45% reduction in violent crime from 2023 to 2024 compared with a 12% increase citywide over the same period. Participant-level data, including crisis activations and referrals to Family Success Planning, is tracked through the organization's internal case management system and reported to funders on a mid-year and final basis, or per each funder's specific reporting schedule.

**Does your organization have any previous or currently pending legal actions against it?**

No.

**Describe why Washington Gas is an appropriate donor and how the local community will benefit from the program.**

Washington Gas's Community Investment Program funds organizations building safer neighborhoods across its DC, Maryland, and Virginia service territory, and Washington Gas delivers gas service to the same Southwest DC blocks where GOODZone's outreach team works every day. Over the past six years, GOODZone's coverage area recorded 249 violent incidents, compared with 601 in the neighboring Navy Yard area, a rate less than half as high despite serving a population with lower income and higher poverty. This funding extends daily outreach coverage in the corridors residents live in and travel through, and that Washington Gas crews and infrastructure serve directly: M Street SE, Buzzard Point, Syphax Gardens, Greenleaf Gardens, and the Waterfront Metro area. A safer Southwest DC means safer working conditions for the employees and infrastructure Washington Gas already maintains in this part of the city, and it strengthens the same neighborhoods where Washington Gas's community investment dollars are meant to have the most direct, visible effect.

In FY25, GOODZone directly engaged 186 community members through violence prevention and trauma response contact; this request sustains and extends that reach. Outputs tracked include the number of daily outreach contacts, crisis activations within 48 hours of a shooting or major conflict, and referrals to Family Success Planning coaches.
