# GOODProjects Grant Qualification Note
Row 200, 2026 dev bible tab: Henry E. Niles Foundation

## Decision: NO-GO (flagged, not drafted)

## Why

The Henry E. Niles Foundation's own FAQ states that **the Foundation is
not currently accepting applications from new organizations**. This is
a hard stop independent of program fit or geography: GOODProjects has no
prior grant history with Niles, so it would be applying as a new
organization into a door that is currently closed. Writing a full
proposal would not change this; the Foundation is not reviewing new-org
applications at all right now.

There is also a secondary, softer geographic concern: although the
Foundation states it makes some grants in other regions and abroad, the
**majority of its grantmaking is concentrated in the northeastern United
States**, and it is not clear DC falls inside that core funding
geography. This would be worth reassessing only if the "new
organizations" gate ever reopens.

## What was found

- **Eligibility:** 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status required.
- **Grant size:** typically $2,500 to $50,000, with larger grants
  considered in unique cases for major-impact programs.
- **Geographic focus:** primarily the northeastern U.S., with some
  grants elsewhere or abroad made only through U.S. tax-exempt
  organizations.
- **Will not fund:** non-501(c)(3) entities, individuals, general
  fundraising drives, endowments, government agencies, or organizations
  that subsist mainly on third-party funding with little effort to
  attract private support.
- **Process:** rolling review, no deadlines, Boards meet 10 times a
  year — but new-organization applications are currently paused.

Source: [Henry E. Niles Foundation — Guidelines](https://www.henryniles.org/guidelines.html),
[Frequently Asked Questions](https://www.henryniles.org/frequently-asked-questions.html)

## Recommendation

Skip for now. This is not a fit/quality problem, it is an intake-status
problem: the Foundation is not reviewing new-organization applications.
Worth periodically rechecking henryniles.org for a status change before
committing drafting time, or asking Neo whether GOODProjects has any
existing contact with the Foundation that predates or falls outside the
current pause.
