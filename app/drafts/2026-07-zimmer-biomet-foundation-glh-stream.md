## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

GOODProjects requests $10,000 from the Zimmer Biomet Foundation to support STREAM programming (Science, Technology, Reading, Engineering, Arts, and Math) inside GOODLearningHub, our afterschool and summer program for youth ages 5 to 14 in Southwest DC's Ward 6. This gift funds hands-on science and technology materials for the students GOODLearningHub already serves daily, directly advancing the Foundation's STEM Education pillar.

## ORGANIZATIONAL OVERVIEW

GOODProjects is a Washington, DC nonprofit, founded in 2016, working to end the cycle of intergenerational poverty through a place-based, two-generation model that pairs children's education and youth development with the economic mobility, food security, health, and stability of the adults around them. We serve residents of James Creek, Syphax Gardens, and Greenleaf, three public housing communities in Southwest DC's Ward 6.

GOODProjects operates with a staff of 30 (11 full-time, 19 part-time) and 50 volunteers, and has invested more than $8 million in Southwest DC since 2016. Our 2024 audit by Desai Company returned an unmodified opinion, signed September 7, 2025.

## COMMUNITY NEED

Youth in Ward 6 public housing attend some of the city's most under-resourced schools, with limited access to hands-on science and technology enrichment outside the school day. GOODLearningHub fills that gap for 73 students currently enrolled (49 boys, 24 girls, ages 6 to 15), with more than 400 youth served daily including summer. STREAM programming gives these students structured, hands-on science, technology, and math activities they would not otherwise get access to.

## PROJECT DESCRIPTION

### Program Anchor
GOODLearningHub, specifically its STREAM component, delivered alongside high-dosage tutoring, Youth Services Advocates, and social-emotional learning at King Greenleaf Recreation Center.

### Target Population
Youth ages 5 to 14 living in Southwest DC public housing, primarily Black youth living below the poverty line.

### Activities and Approach
This gift funds STREAM materials and supplies for GOODLearningHub's existing afterschool and GOODCamps summer sessions: science kits, coding and robotics materials, and hands-on math manipulatives used during the program's 110 in-person program dates each year. No new staff or new program is required; the funding extends materials capacity for a program already running.

## OUTCOMES AND EVALUATION

GOODLearningHub tracks tutoring sessions, hours of active learning, and skills mastered through its existing data systems (IXL platform, CTDC App). In FY25, the program delivered 264 tutoring sessions, 1,313 or more hours of active learning, and 1,912 skills mastered across core subjects.

## EQUITY STATEMENT

We operate on the principle that economic mobility requires addressing both skills gaps and systemic barriers at the same time. We practice equity by removing barriers rather than asking children to overcome them. We recruit staff from the communities we serve and design programming with families, not for them.
