## PART A — LETTER OF INTENT (LOI)

### Opening Paragraph — Mission and Ask

GOODProjects operates Family Success Planning (FSP), a coaching model that pairs every enrolled family with a dedicated coach to build financial stability and economic mobility. We respectfully request $5,000 from TD Bank's Community Sponsorship Program to support financial literacy coaching and wealth-building tools for families enrolled in FSP in Southwest DC's Ward 6.

### The Problem We Are Solving

Median household income in the Ward 6 public housing communities GOODProjects serves is approximately $14,000 a year, well below the threshold for financial stability in the DC region. Without dedicated coaching, families cycling in and out of crisis rarely get the sustained support needed to build savings, access benefits, or move into stable employment. FSP addresses this directly: each family receives a customized plan covering workforce readiness, financial literacy coaching, entrepreneurship training, and wealth-building tools, including whole-life insurance policies that build intergenerational assets.

### Our Approach

Every family enrolled in FSP is paired with a dedicated Family Success Coach who conducts a comprehensive needs assessment and builds a customized Family Success Plan. Coaching covers immediate stabilization (emergency assistance, benefits navigation) alongside economic mobility work: job placement support, financial literacy coaching, entrepreneurship training, and access to wealth-building tools. Families are supported for as long as they need coaching to reach their goals, not on a fixed timeline.

### Why We Are the Right Partner

GOODProjects has served Southwest DC for nearly a decade. We have invested $8 million in Ward 6 families, earned recognition from Forbes, CBS 60 Minutes, and NBC Nightly News, and built lasting partnerships with the DC Department of Employment Services, DC Parks and Recreation, and more than 20 community organizations.

### The Ask

We respectfully request $5,000 to support financial literacy coaching and wealth-building tools for families enrolled in Family Success Planning in Southwest DC's Ward 6.

## PART B — FULL APPLICATION

### Organization Description

GOODProjects is a Washington, DC nonprofit, founded in 2016, working to end the cycle of intergenerational poverty through a place-based, two-generation model. We pair children's education and youth development with the economic mobility, food security, health, and stability of the adults around them. We serve residents of James Creek, Syphax Gardens, and Greenleaf, three public housing communities in Southwest DC's Ward 6, where median household income is approximately $14,000 per year.

### Community Served

We serve residents of James Creek, Syphax Gardens, and Greenleaf, three public housing communities in Southwest DC's Ward 6. Approximately 95% of participants identify as Black or African American. Median household income is approximately $14,000 per year.

### Program Description

Family Success Planning is GOODProjects' entry point into its full continuum of care: the cycle of intergenerational poverty cannot be interrupted without first stabilizing the home. Each family is paired with a dedicated Family Success Coach who builds a customized plan covering immediate stabilization (emergency food, housing, and utility assistance; benefits navigation for Medicaid, SNAP, and TANF) and economic mobility (workforce readiness, job placement, financial literacy coaching, entrepreneurship training, and wealth-building tools, including whole-life insurance policies). School-aged children in enrolled families are automatically connected to GOODLearningHub. This request funds financial literacy coaching materials and wealth-building tools specifically, the economic mobility component of FSP's work.
