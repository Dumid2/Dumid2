## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Henry E. Niles Foundation funds Economic Self-Sufficiency work, including job training, entrepreneurship support, mentoring, and micro-credit initiatives. GOODProjects requests $22,550 to fund 10 paid internship placements through JUMPSTART, the workforce pathway that has already produced two Ward 6 entrepreneurs through its Nasdaq Women's Financial Literacy Program cohort. This grant would extend that same job-training and mentoring model to 10 additional young adults during the 2026-2027 program year.

## ORGANIZATIONAL OVERVIEW

GOODProjects is a Washington, DC nonprofit (founded 2016) working to end the cycle of intergenerational poverty through a place-based, two-generation model, pairing children's education and youth development with the economic mobility, food security, health, and stability of the adults around them, so that progress in one generation reinforces the next. We serve residents of James Creek, Syphax Gardens, and Greenleaf, three public housing communities in Southwest DC's Ward 6, where the median household income is approximately $14,000.

GOODProjects operates with a staff of 30 (11 full-time, 19 part-time) and 50 volunteers. The organization has invested more than $8 million in Southwest DC since 2016. Our CEO Darius Baxter (Forbes 30 Under 30, 2023 Washingtonian of the Year) brings deep personal connection to the mission alongside executive leadership experience. Our 2024 audit by Desai Company returned an unmodified opinion, signed September 7, 2025.

## COMMUNITY NEED

Southwest DC's Ward 6 public housing communities, James Creek, Syphax Gardens, and Greenleaf, are among the city's most resource-constrained neighborhoods. The families GOODProjects serves face overlapping, mutually reinforcing challenges: limited employment pathways, fragile housing, food insecurity, underfunded schools, health disparities, and the compounding trauma of community violence. Addressing any one of these in isolation does not produce lasting change. GOODProjects addresses them together.

Ward 6 saw a 25% increase in gun-related incidents in 2024, a trend closely tied to the same economic exclusion that leaves young adults without a structured path from school to steady employment. Without paid, supervised work experience, GOODLearningHub alumni risk losing the academic and social-emotional gains built over years of afterschool investment the moment they age out of youth programming.

## PROJECT DESCRIPTION

### Program Anchor

JUMPSTART, GOODProjects' youth workforce development initiative, directly matches the Foundation's Economic Self-Sufficiency focus area: job training, entrepreneurship support, mentoring, and a proven pathway from training to paid work.

### Target Population

GOODProjects serves Black families living in Ward 6 public housing, James Creek, Syphax Gardens, and Greenleaf. JUMPSTART serves GOODLearningHub alumni ages 14 to 24 with an active Family Success Planning family connection, young adults at risk of idling and economic exclusion after aging out of afterschool programming.

### Activities and Approach

JUMPSTART moves participants through four stages: proactive outreach and intake with a dedicated coach, a 40-hour Career Launchpad covering resume building, interviewing, professional communication, financial literacy, and workplace etiquette, a six-week paid internship placement with a GOODZone Business Partner at $15 per hour for 20 hours per week, and a 90-day retention period with a dedicated Placement Coordinator managing the transition to permanent employment. This request funds Stage 3 internship placements for 10 participants.

## OUTCOMES AND EVALUATION

GOODProjects tracks participation and outcomes across all programs using Salesforce CRM and program-specific data tools. For JUMPSTART, key indicators include Career Launchpad completion, internship completion, employment or post-secondary enrollment within 90 days, and internship-to-permanent-hire conversion. Data is reviewed quarterly by program leads and annually in our impact report.

### Committed Outcomes for This Grant

| Output | 10 JUMPSTART participants enrolled in paid internship placements |
|---|---|
| Short-term | 90% complete the 40-hour Career Launchpad training |
| Medium-term | 85% complete the six-week paid internship |
| Long-term | 40% convert from internship to permanent hire, consistent with JUMPSTART's documented conversion rate |

GOODProjects' JUMPSTART model has already demonstrated the kind of economic self-sufficiency outcome the Foundation prioritizes: in 2025, GOODProjects' Nasdaq Women's Financial Literacy Program cohort produced 15 completers, two of whom launched registered businesses in Ward 6.

## EQUITY STATEMENT

We operate on the principle that economic mobility requires addressing both skills gaps and systemic barriers simultaneously. We practice equity by eliminating barriers rather than asking people to overcome them. Our hiring, programming, and partnerships prioritize those with lived experience of the challenges we address. We actively recruit staff and board members from the communities we serve, recognizing that proximity to the problem creates proximity to the solution. We do not design programs for communities; we design them with communities.
