GOODProjects Grant Qualification Note
Goldman Sachs 2026 Community Development Champions Grant (Change Makers
Initiative / Equity Builders Initiative)
Source: LinkedIn post by Thomas Claffey (Philanthropy Solutions Group),
linking to goldmansachs.com/community-transformation/community-development-grants

## Decision: NO-GO (flagged, not drafted)

## Why

The program's eligibility is restricted to organizations that operate
primarily in specific counties in three states:

- **New York:** New York, Bronx, Kings, Richmond, or Queens counties
- **New Jersey:** Hudson or Essex counties
- **Utah:** Salt Lake, Summit, or Tooele counties

GOODProjects' primary service area is Southwest DC public housing
communities (James Creek, Syphax Gardens, Greenleaf — Ward 6). GOODProjects
has no operating presence in any of the eligible NY, NJ, or UT counties.
This fails Part 7 criterion 1 (geographic fit) outright — the same failure
mode as the Lubrizol opportunity.

## What was found

- **Two tracks:** a one-time general operating grant (Change Makers
  Initiative) and a multi-year general operating grant (Equity Builders
  Initiative), both flexible/unrestricted funding.
- **Focus areas:** affordable housing, community services, small business
  growth, and neighborhood revitalization — otherwise a strong program
  match for GOODProjects' housing/wealth-building/community-development
  work, if not for the location restriction.
- **Deadline:** July 24, 2026, 11:59 PM EST.
- **Past grantees cited:** Brooklyn Public Library, DataKind, Rising Tide
  Capital, The HOPE Program — all NY/NJ-region organizations, consistent
  with the stated geographic restriction.
- Full RFP was not independently reviewed (LinkedIn post + comments only);
  the geographic restriction was confirmed by two separate commenters
  quoting the same eligibility language, not yet cross-checked directly
  against the Goldman Sachs application portal.

## Recommendation

Skip this opportunity — do not spend drafting time on it. Flag to Neo only
if GOODProjects has (or is considering) an operating footprint in one of
the eligible counties; otherwise there is no plausible fit. If a future
Goldman Sachs cycle drops the geographic restriction or opens a DC region,
worth revisiting given the strong focus-area match.
