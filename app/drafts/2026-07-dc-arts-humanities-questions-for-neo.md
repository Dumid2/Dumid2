## 2. PROGRAM DESIGN — decisions only Neo can make

**Q3. Confirm the grade band: 6th-8th grade, classified under the Dance discipline.**
This is my recommendation (it lets us keep Step Afrika! at Arena Stage as the field trip, since
Step Afrika is a dance company, while Theatre would only be eligible for grades 6-12). Before
finalizing, we need GOODLearningHub's actual 6th-8th grade headcount — specifically, how many of
the 73 enrolled students attend Jefferson Middle School — to confirm 30 participants genuinely
exist in this one band.

**Q4. Weekday matinee (school-day release) or weekend matinee?**
GOODLearningHub is an afterschool program. A weekday noon matinee means pulling students out of
regular school hours at Jefferson Middle School, which likely needs that school's own
administration to authorize — nobody has asked them yet. A weekend matinee (Arena Stage runs
Saturday/Sunday shows too) would avoid that dependency entirely and keep the whole trip inside
GOODLearningHub's own program hours. This is a real fork in how we plan logistics, so it's worth
deciding before looping in Phileke and Stephanie.

**Q5. Confirm the budget stays at $5,500, not a larger ask.**
An internal Abacus review generated an alternate budget requesting the full $30,000 cap, with
several unsubstantiated line items (an $8,500 unnamed teaching artist fee, no ticket line at all).
I recommended against it — the request-to-scope ratio (30 students, one field trip) doesn't
support that amount, and CAH's own scoring rewards a "detailed, accurate, feasible" budget over a
larger, less-supported one. Flagging this here so Neo has visibility into that decision, not just
the model's word for it.
