## Resolved: IRS-filed mission statement

The playbook repeatedly flags the mission statement as **[confirm exact
wording against approved boilerplate]**. Form 990 (2024), Part I, gives the
legally filed version:

> "GOODProjects designs opportunities for youth and their families to live
> fulfilling lives, free from poverty and violence with improvements to
> their health and wellness."

**This is different wording from the mission statement in
`app/org_profile/01-org-identity.md`** ("To empower underserved families to
break cycles of intergenerational poverty by providing resources, tools,
and holistic support to achieve financial independence and build
generational wealth."). Both are legitimate — the 990 version is the
IRS-of-record language; the org-identity version reads as the
communications/marketing mission. For compliance-heavy funders (DC
government, federal) who may cross-check the 990, prefer the 990 wording or
note both. Confirm with Neo/Darius which to lead with per funder type.

The FY2024 audit (Note 1) gives a third, longer variant: "committed to
providing opportunities daily for at-risk youth and their families to live
fulfilling lives, free from poverty, violence and disease." Also worth
reconciling with Darius/Neo rather than picking one silently.
