## Narrative highlights (reusable structure for similar projects)

- **Honoraria ($5,100):** $1,200 for 8 community narrator participants
  ($150 each); $600 for 8 BJF graduate co-participants ($75 each); $500
  facilitation honorarium for the Humanities Scholar's orientation
  workshop prep/delivery; $300 for digital-archive technical
  setup/hosting; $900 for the Humanities Scholar's base session fees (3
  sessions × $300); remainder folds into the separate Humanities Scholar
  Honorarium line.
- **Salary/Wages ($7,500):** $5,000 for Project Coordinator **Cole
  Bradshaw** at ~20% FTE over 10 months (logistics, participant
  recruitment, release forms, venue coordination with King Greenleaf,
  transcript oversight, grant reporting); $1,700 for contracted
  filmmaker post-production; $800 for supplemental archival-grade audio
  equipment rental.
- **Travel ($0):** all activities held at King Greenleaf Recreation
  Center — no travel budget needed, a reusable line for any
  King-Greenleaf-based project.
- **Supplies ($800):** archival-grade USB drives/backup media, consent
  forms, metadata prep materials.
- **Publicity ($700):** 500+ flyers/posters at Southwest DC community
  hubs (churches, barbershops, libraries, senior centers, King
  Greenleaf); social media; printed launch-event programs.
- **Facilities Rental ($1,000):** King Greenleaf space rental, ~$125/session
  × 6 sessions + $250 for the launch event (full commercial rate as a
  ceiling; actual cost may be lower via the community partnership).
- **Evaluation ($400):** pre/post participant surveys, launch-event
  audience feedback, attendance tracking, final impact report.
- **Other ($9,500):** $8,000 contracted Videographer/Audio Engineer (~18
  hrs filming six 60–90 min sessions + ~30 hrs editing a 15–20 min
  documentary); $1,500 professional verbatim transcription (~12 hrs
  audio at ~$2.08/minute).
- **Humanities Scholar Honorarium ($2,500):** Excellence in Changemaking
  Institute (ECI) — **Phillipe M. Cunningham** — ~50 hours: co-developing
  interview frameworks, leading the orientation workshop, participating
  in 3+ of 6 sessions, historical contextualization of transcripts,
  interpretive archive notes, advising on documentary editorial framing.
