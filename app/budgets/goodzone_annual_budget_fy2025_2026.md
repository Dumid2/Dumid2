### A2. Field Staff — Credible Messengers & Outreach Workers — $180,000

20 total staff delivering daily street-level presence in hotspot
corridors: M Street SE, Buzzard Point, Syphax Gardens, Greenleaf Gardens,
Waterfront Metro area.

| Position | # Staff | Annual Salary | Total |

### A3. Crisis Intervention Specialists — $60,000

3 specialists at $20,000/year, responding within 48 hours of any shooting
or major conflict alongside CWV (Community Wellness for Violence) and FSP
coaches.

### A4. Fringe Benefits — $79,200

22% of $360,000 base personnel cost (FICA, health insurance, workers'
comp, unemployment insurance).

**Total personnel headcount: 28 staff** (3 leadership + 20 field + 3 crisis + 2 unaccounted — verify exact count against org chart before citing "25+" or "28" in a narrative).

## D. Technology & Communications — $15,000

Laptops/tablets ($4,800), software/platform subscriptions ($5,100),
website/digital communications ($3,552), printing/media ($1,548). Funds
the GOODZone data dashboard layering MPD PSA 105 crime data against
program activity.

## E. Indirect Costs — $40,800

10% of $408,000 direct costs — explicitly pitched in the narrative as
**below the 15–20% nonprofit sector benchmark**, a good line to use when
a funder is indirect-cost-sensitive.

## Evaluation framework (six outcome domains, useful for any GOODZone logic model)

1. Violence reduction (MPD PSA 105 data)
2. Participant stabilization (FSP referrals)
3. Crisis response (48-hour activations)
4. Employment placement (via SWBID)
5. Program reach
6. Community perception
