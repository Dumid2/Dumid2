# GOODProjects Grant Writer

A working reference for producing high-quality GOODProjects grant submissions fast, and for qualifying opportunities before time is spent on them. The detailed reference lives in **PLAYBOOK.md** (in this skill folder) — open it when drafting, reviewing, budgeting, researching a funder, or deciding whether to pursue an opportunity.

## The core principle (90 / 10)

GOODProjects submissions are **~90% stable boilerplate** (the canon: mission, need, programs, theory of change, equity, evaluation, staffing, partnerships). That part is reusable and is what makes drafting fast. **Award rate is won in the other 10% — the customization:** real budget figures, proof metrics matched to the funder's domain, a tailored executive summary, and a clean strip of machine artifacts and contradictions.

## Workflow for any opportunity

1. **Qualify first** (PLAYBOOK Part 7). Geographic fit + program match + entity eligibility + **compliance fit** (can GOODProjects actually meet the funder's procedural load — SAM.gov/UEI, certifications, negotiated indirect rate — inside the timeline?) + capacity/timeline + strategic value. For any foundation, pull the funder's IRS Form 990-PF (three to five years) before qualifying — it shows real past grantees and award ranges, not just stated priorities. Where possible, contact the Program Officer before drafting to confirm fit. If it fails 1–4, flag it as a no-go to Neo before drafting.
2. **Build from the canon** (PLAYBOOK Part 2). Paste-and-trim the reusable sections.
3. **Customize hard** (PLAYBOOK Parts 3–4). Fill every real figure, pick funder-matched proof points, tailor the exec summary to this funder's language and priorities.
4. **Apply craft discipline** (PLAYBOOK Part 9). Needs statement on relevance/data/sparing emotion/urgency with no circular reasoning; SMART(IE) objectives; every budget line calculated, explained, and tied to implementation (in-kind included); one consistent point of view; logos/ethos/pathos balance; every statistic followed by an explanatory sentence (never a dropped statistic); cross-sectional coherence across needs, objectives, methods, evaluation, and budget.
5. **Humanize the draft** (PLAYBOOK Part 8). Mandatory before export. Run the draft → audit → final loop: strip AI-writing tells (significance inflation, promotional puffery, "-ing" padding, vague attributions, copula avoidance, rule-of-three, synonym cycling, filler, chatbot framing, hedging disclaimers) and remove every em and en dash. If a standalone humanizer-style skill is enabled, load and run it for the full rule set; Part 8 carries the condensed, grant-adapted version so the core rules always apply even if it is not.
6. **Run the QA gate** (PLAYBOOK Part 6) before export. Mandatory, not optional — covers placeholders, machine artifacts, reconciled numbers, the humanize check, and the craft-discipline checks from Part 9.
7. **Deliver PDF alongside the editable source** (Word/Excel) every time.
8. **After the decision.** If awarded a federal grant, confirm reporting ownership before funds are drawn down (PLAYBOOK Part 10: time-and-effort documentation, reporting calendar, Single Audit threshold, subrecipient monitoring). If declined, work the rejection systematically (PLAYBOOK Part 11) rather than treating it as a dead end — check funder-specific resubmission rules before assuming a simple resubmit is welcome.

## Non-negotiables (quick gate before anything leaves)

- **No unfilled placeholders.** Every `[X]`, `[INSERT]`, `TBD`, or blank is replaced with a real, verified figure. (~84% of past files shipped with these unfilled — the single most common defect.)
- **No machine artifacts.** Strip `internal_name`/`funder_language` tags, underscore slugs, Run IDs, and Drive-context appendices.
- **Numbers reconcile.** The headline ask equals the budget total; metrics don't contradict each other; the ask is within the funder's cap.
- **Dates are valid** and fall inside the project period (no completion-before-start, no malformed years).
- **Staffing roster is current.** Do not name the former "Cara Adams, Development Manager."
- **Attachments are uploaded files, not Google Drive links.**
- **Certifications are signed by the correct Authorized Official** and the typed name matches the signature.
- **Verify-before-reuse figures are confirmed against source** (see PLAYBOOK Appendix B) — e.g. $14,700 average income, "DC's #1 tutoring hub," "zero gun incidents," the address, the ward claim.
- **No AI-writing tells.** Run the humanize pass (PLAYBOOK Part 8): no significance inflation or empty superlatives, no vague attributions, no chatbot framing or hedging disclaimers, and no em or en dashes anywhere in the final text.
- **Craft discipline holds** (PLAYBOOK Part 9): SMART(IE) objectives, non-circular needs statement, every budget line calculated and justified, one consistent point of view, clean grammar, every section telling the same story, every statistic followed by an explanatory sentence.
- **Opportunities are qualified on compliance fit, not just mission fit** (PLAYBOOK Part 7).
